Pearls of Sluze

An algebraic family and a polar variant whose parameter choices produce bead- or pearl-like loops along the x-axis.

Pearls of Sluze Cartesian equation:
\[ y^{m} = k\, x^{n} (a-x)^{b} \]
Variant polar equation:
\[ r = a\,\dfrac{\cos(\theta)}{\sin^{n}(\theta)} \]

History and Applications

The Pearls of Sluze1,2,3 are a family of algebraic plane curves studied by the Belgian mathematician and cleric René François de Sluse (1622–1685) between 1657 and 1698. De Sluse, a canon at Liège Cathedral who corresponded with Blaise Pascal and Christiaan Huygens on geometry and analysis, explored these higher-degree curves as part of the 17th-century development of analytic geometry following Descartes. Blaise Pascal named the family the “Pearls of Sluze” because certain parameter choices produce bead- or pearl-like shapes strung along the x-axis. The curves generalize simpler forms such as the teardrop curve.

There is a somewhat visually similar curve using a polar, trigonometric formulation that is included as an option in this code, though not truly named “Pearls of Sluze”. This polar form generates paired, bulbous profiles rather than a closed “pearl”, with each half bearing a similarity to the teardrop curve. Neither form has known practical applications in engineering or the natural sciences other than as decorative motifs.

AutoLISP Code

(defun C:PEARLSOFSLUZE (/ basept xaxisangle cd sa sb sm om os divcount cosangle sinangle x0 y0 plineents segcount baseinfo divpt xspan paramstep restartflag
                          dcl_id dcl_pos dcl_opts id iq dcl opt kw mode dpr dct dpt span rval eq_display xc yc skm a1 a2 x1 y1 x2 y2 fa fb fc fd fe ff)
  (setq dcl_id (load_dialog "PEARLSOFSLUZE.dcl") dcl_pos '(1680 0))
  (if (< dcl_id 0) (exit))
  (if (not (new_dialog "PEARLSOFSLUZE" dcl_id "" dcl_pos)) (exit))
  (setq dcl_opts '("Algebraic form" "Polar form"))
  (start_list "list_col") (mapcar 'add_list dcl_opts) (end_list)
  (setq kw 0)
  (set_tile "list_col" "0")
  (action_tile "list_col" "(setq kw (atoi $value))")
  (start_dialog)
  (unload_dialog dcl_id)
  (cond
   ((eq kw 0)
    (setq id "Pearls of Sluze"
          iq "yᵐ = k·xⁿ·(a-x)ᵇ"
          dcl nil
          opt nil
          mode 'param
          dpr "# of divisions in pearl"
          dct 200
          dpt '(2.0 0.0)
          span nil
          kw (ca id iq dcl opt kw mode dpr dct dpt span)
          fa (get_real 2 "a " 4.0)
          fb (get_real 2 "k " 2.0)
          fc (get_real 2 "m (power of y): " 4.0)
          fd (get_real 2 "n (power of x): " 2.0)
          fe (get_real 2 "b (power of a-x): " 3.0)
          ff (get_keyword 0 "Positive Negative" "\nSweep in Positive or Negative direction?" "Positive")
          paramstep (/ fa divcount)
          paramstep (if (eq ff "Positive") paramstep (- paramstep))
          eq_display (strcat "\na" (rtos fa 2 4) ", k= " (rtos fb 2 4) ", m= " (rtos fc 2 4) ", n= " (rtos fd 2 4) ", b= " (rtos fe 2 4))
          xc '(eval a2)
          yc '(alt_expt (* fb (alt_expt (eval a2) fd nil) (alt_expt (- fa (eval a2)) fe nil)) (/ 1.0 fc) nil)))
   ((eq kw 1)   
    (setq id "Pearls of Sluze"
          iq "r = a·cos(𝛉)/sinⁿ(𝛉)"
          dcl nil
          opt nil
          mode 'polar
          dpr "# of divisions in right-half of curve"
          dct 360
          dpt nil
          span 180
          kw (ca id iq dcl opt kw mode dpr dct dpt span)
          fa (get_real 2 "a (scale)" 1.0)
          fb (get_real 2 "n (sine exponent)" 0.5)
          eq_display (strcat "\na=" (rtos fa 2 4) ", n= " (rtos fb 2 4))
          rval '(* fa (/ (cos (eval a2)) (avoid_zero (alt_expt (sin (eval a2)) fb nil))))
          xc '(* (eval rval) (cos (eval a2)))
          yc '(* (eval rval) (sin (eval a2)))
          maxdraw (get_real 2 "Maximum x and y values allowed?" 40))))
  (setq skm (if (eq kw 0) '(T nil T) '(nil T T))
        a1 0
        a2 a1
        x1 (eval xc)
        y1 (eval yc)
        a2 '(+ a1 paramstep)
        x2 '(eval xc)
        y2 '(eval yc))
  (cc))
(C:PEARLSOFSLUZE)
