Parabolic Spiral

A member of the Archimedean family with polar equation \(r=a+b\sqrt{\theta}\). The special case \(a=0\) is Fermat’s spiral. Radius grows with the square root of polar angle; in the Fermat case the area between successive turns is constant.

Parabolic Spiral polar equation:
\[ r = a + b\sqrt{\theta} \]

History and Applications

The parabolic spiral, with polar equation \(r=a+b\sqrt{\theta}\), is a member of the Archimedean family of plane spirals. Pierre de Fermat described the special case \(r=b\sqrt{\theta}\) (when \(a=0\)) in 1636 in a letter to Marin Mersenne while examining areas and tangents of curves.1 The general offset form was studied by Jacob Bernoulli in 1691.2 A property useful to engineers follows from the polar area formula \(A=\frac{1}{2}\int r^{2}\,d\theta\). For the basic Fermat case the area between successive turns remains constant, independent of starting angle, because the \(r^{2}\) term grows linearly with \(\theta\). The offset \(a\) shifts the starting radius but preserves the overall spiral character.

Real-life applications in engineering and the natural sciences are documented primarily for the Fermat special case and extend to the general parabolic spiral. In electrical engineering the constant-area property is used in variable-capacitor designs where rotating plates follow the spiral to produce linear capacitance change with angle.3 In renewable-energy engineering the same spiral guides efficient heliostat-mirror layouts in concentrated solar-power plants to optimize light concentration.2 In the natural sciences the curve models phyllotaxis, the spiral arrangement of seeds or florets in sunflowers and daisies, where successive elements follow approximate parabolic spirals.

Also see Fermat Spiral.

AutoLISP Code

(defun C:PARASPIRAL (/ basept xaxisangle cd sa sb sm om os divcount cosangle sinangle x0 y0 plineents segcount baseinfo divpt xstep paramstep
                       xspan restartflag id iq dcl opt kw mode dpr dct dpt span eq_display xc yc skm a1 a2 x1 y1 x2 y2 fa fb rval)
  (setq id "Parabolic Spiral"
        iq "r=a+b·sqrt(𝛉)"
        dcl nil
        opt nil
        kw nil
        mode 'polar
        dpr "# of divisions around circle"
        dct 360
        dpt nil
        span 360
        kw (ca id iq dcl opt kw mode dpr dct dpt span)
        fa (get_real 2 "a (offset radius)" 0.0)
        fb (get_real 2 "b (scale)" 1.0)
        eq_display (strcat "\na=" (rtos fa 2 4) ", b=" (rtos fb 2 4))
        rval '(+ fa (* fb (alt_expt (eval a2) 0.5 nil)))
        xc '(* (eval rval) (cos (eval a2)))
        yc '(* (eval rval) (sin (eval a2)))
        skm '(T T nil)
        a1 0
        a2 a1
        x1 (eval xc)
        y1 (eval yc)
        a2 '(+ a1 paramstep)
        x2 '(eval xc)
        y2 '(eval yc))
  (cc))
(C:PARASPIRAL)
