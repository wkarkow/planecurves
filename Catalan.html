Catalan Curve

Polar curve r = a / (1 − θ²) arising as a circular glissette with acusped origin and asymptotic branches as θ approaches ±1.

Catalan Curve polar equation:
\[ r = \frac{a}{1 - \theta^{2}} \]

History and Applications

Catalan's curve1,2 was introduced by the Franco-Belgian mathematician Eugène Charles Catalan (1814–1894) in his 1856 paper "Note sur la théorie des roulettes."2 It addresses the problem of finding a curve that rolls without slipping on a straight line while a fixed point in its plane traces a circle tangent to that line. This classifies it as a circular roulette or glissette. The curve also satisfies the condition where the arc length s = ρθ, with initial condition ρ = 1 at θ = 0. Differentiating it leads to a differential equation whose solutions include the circle and Catalan's curve as the nontrivial solution. It is additionally the evolute of the tractrix spiral.1

Real-life applications in engineering and the natural sciences are limited. The curve appears in theoretical studies of kinematics involving rolling curves and roulettes, but no widespread practical uses in mechanisms, optics, or other applied fields were identified beyond its mathematical significance in curve theory.

AutoLISP Code

(defun C:CATALAN (/ basept xaxisangle cd sa sb sm om os divcount cosangle sinangle x0 y0 plineents segcount baseinfo paramstep
                    restartflag id iq dcl opt kw mode dpr dct dpt span eq_display xc yc skm a1 a2 x1 y1 x2 y2 maxdraw rval fa)
  (setq id "Catalan Curve"
        iq "r = a / (1 - θ²)"
        dcl nil
        opt nil
        kw nil
        mode 'polar
        dpr "# of divisions around circle"
        dct 360
        dpt nil
        span 360
        kw (ca id iq dcl opt kw mode dpr dct dpt span)
        fa (get_real 6 "\nCatalan curve: r=a/(1-θ²), a (scale)" 1.0)
        eq_display (strcat "\na=" (rtos fa 2 4))
        rval '(* fa (/ 1.0 (- 1.0 (* (eval a2) (eval a2)))))
        xc '(* (eval rval) (cos (eval a2)))
        yc '(* (eval rval) (sin (eval a2)))
        maxdraw (get_real 2 "Maximum x and y values allowed?" 20.0)
        skm '(T nil T)
        a1 0
        a2 a1
        x1 (eval xc)
        y1 (eval yc)
        a2 '(+ a1 paramstep)
        x2 '(eval xc)
        y2 '(eval yc))
  (cc))
(C:CATALAN)
