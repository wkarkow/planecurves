Semicubical Parabola

A cuspidal cubic, the first nontrivial algebraic curve to have its arc length computed.

Semicubical Parabola Cartesian equation:
\[ a \cdot y^{2} = x^{3} \]
Semicubical Parabola parametric equations:
\[ x = a \cdot t^{2},\quad y = \pm a \cdot t^{3} \]

History and Applications

The semicubical parabola1,2,3, also known as Neile’s parabola or the cuspidal cubic, was discovered by English mathematician William Neile (1637–1670) in 1657. Neile computed its arc length, making it the first nontrivial algebraic curve to be rectified; John Wallis published the method in 1659, giving full credit to Neile.1 In 1687 Gottfried Wilhelm Leibniz posed the problem of finding the curve along which a particle descending under gravity covers equal vertical distances in equal times; Christiaan Huygens demonstrated that the semicubical parabola satisfies this isochronous property. The curve is also the evolute of the ordinary parabola.

In classical mechanics it models this specific isochronous descent path under uniform gravity. The surface of revolution obtained by rotating the semicubical parabola about its axis forms the neiloid, used in dendrometry to calculate volumes of tree trunks that exhibit negative curvature.4

AutoLISP Code

(defun C:SEMICUBPARAB (/ basept xaxisangle cd sa sb sm om os divcount cosangle sinangle x0 y0 plineents segcount baseinfo paramstep
                         divpt xspan restartflag id iq dcl opt kw mode dpr dct dpt span eq_display xc yc skm a1 a2 x1 y1 x2 y2 fa)
  (setq id "Semicubical Parabola"
        iq "a·y²=x³"
        dcl nil
        opt nil
        kw nil
        mode 'param
        dpr "# of divisions to x-span of division point"
        dct 100
        dpt '(1.0 0.0)
        span nil
        kw (ca id iq dcl opt kw mode dpr dct dpt span)
        fa (get_real 2 "a (scale)" 1.0)
        eq_display (strcat "\na=" (rtos fa 2 4))
        xc '(eval a2)
        yc '(alt_expt (/ (expt (eval a2) 3.0) fa) 0.5 nil)
        paramstep (/ xspan divcount)
        skm '(T nil T)
        a1 0
        a2 a1
        x1 (eval xc)
        y1 (eval yc)
        a2 '(+ a1 paramstep)
        x2 '(eval xc)
        y2 '(eval yc))
  (cc))
(C:SEMICUBPARAB)
