Quintic Heart Curve

A polar curve that generates a recognizable heart shape through a compact trigonometric expression in the radius.

Quintic Heart Curve polar equation:
\[ r = \dfrac{a \cdot \sin(\theta)}{1 + \cos(\theta)\cos(2\theta)} \]
Equivalent Cartesian quintic:
\[ y^{5} + 2x^{2}y^{3} + 5x^{4}y - 2a(x^{2}+y^{2})^{2} + a^{2}y(x^{2}+y^{2}) = 0 \]

History and Applications

The Quintic Heart Curve is a polar curve that generates a recognizable heart shape through a compact trigonometric expression in the radius. It belongs to the broader class of recreational and computational curves explored for visual symmetry and a closed-form polar representation. Heart curves in general illustrate how polar equations can produce cusped, bilaterally symmetric figures that align with the drafting needs of engineers, architects, and designers using tools such as AutoCAD.

The name refers to algebraic degree. MathWorld collects equations for several other heart-shaped curves (cardioid and the numbered first through seventh forms) but does not list this polar equation.1 MathCurve’s ornamental-curve page likewise treats other decorative hearts, not this form.2 The source of this polar equation is unknown. It may have been written by the author, or taken from a source whose attribution has been lost. The author will gladly credit a prior publication if a reader can point to one.

Real-life applications in engineering and the natural sciences for this exact quintic variant were not identified in the literature. General heart-shaped geometries appear in cartographic projections (such as the Bonne projection for equal-area maps of continents) and in mathematical visualization software, but no documented uses specific to this polar form were found.

AutoLISP Code

(defun C:QUINTICHEART (/ basept xaxisangle cd sa sb sm om os divcount cosangle sinangle x0 y0 plineents segcount baseinfo divpt xstep paramstep
                         restartflag rval xc yc skm a1 a2 x1 y1 x2 y2 eq_display fa maxdraw)
  (setq id "Quintic Heart Curve"
        iq "r=a·sin(θ)/(1+cos(θ)·cos(2θ))"
        dcl nil
        opt nil
        kw nil
        mode 'polar
        dpr "# of divisions around circle"
        dct 360
        dpt nil
        span 360
        kw (ca id iq dcl opt kw mode dpr dct dpt span)
        fa (get_real 2 "a (scale)" 1.0)
        eq_display (strcat "\na=" (rtos fa 2 4))
        rval '(/ (* fa (sin (eval a2))) (avoid_zero (+ 1.0 (* (cos (eval a2)) (cos (* 2.0 (eval a2)))))))
        xc '(* (eval rval) (cos (eval a2)))
        yc '(* (eval rval) (sin (eval a2)))
        maxdraw (get_real 2 "Maximum x and y values allowed?" (* fa 20.0))
        skm '(T T T)
        a1 0
        a2 a1
        x1 (eval xc)
        y1 (eval yc)
        a2 '(+ a1 paramstep)
        x2 '(eval xc)
        y2 '(eval yc))
  (cc))
(C:QUINTICHEART)
