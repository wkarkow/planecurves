Arbelos

A classical geometric figure formed by three semicircles sharing a common diameter, known as the shoemaker's knife, with notable Archimedean properties including the Pappus chain.

Arbelos figure:
Three semicircles sharing a common diameter (shoemaker's knife figure)
Parametric equations (each semicircle):
\[ x(\theta) = x_c + r \cos(\theta),\quad y(\theta) = y_c + r \sin(\theta) \quad (\theta \in [0,\pi]) \]

History and Applications

The Arbelos, meaning “shoemaker’s knife” in Greek, is a classical geometric figure introduced by Archimedes in his Book of Lemmas around 250 BCE1. The name reflects its resemblance to the blade used by ancient cobblers. The arbelos is formed by three semicircles: two smaller ones nestled inside a larger semicircle, all sharing a common baseline. Archimedes explored several properties of this figure, including the area enclosed by the semicircles and the construction of inscribed circles now known as Archimedean circles. He proved that the area of the arbelos equals that of a circle whose diameter is the perpendicular from the point where the two smaller semicircles meet to the arc of the large semicircle2. A sequence of circles, each tangent to the two smaller semicircles and its immediate neighbors, can be inscribed within the arbelos. This configuration is known as the Pappus chain and has centers lying on an ellipse3.

Real-life applications include the design of mechanical components that need to distribute stress evenly in engineering and the modeling of wave patterns in optics for lens and mirror surfaces4.

(defun C:ARBELOS (/ basept xaxisangle cd sa sb sm om os divcount cosangle sinangle x0 y0 id iq dcl opt kw mode dpr dct dpt span fa fb fc eq_display
                  sr a1 a2 d3 xc yc x1 y1 x2 y2 e1 sn a3 x3 y3)
  (setq id "Arbelos Curve"
        iq "Each semicircle: x(θ)=r·cos(θ)+x₀, y(θ)=r·sin(θ)+y₀ for θ∈[0,π]"
        dcl nil
        opt nil
        kw nil
        mode 'polar
        dpr "# of segments per semicircle"
        dct 60
        dpt nil
        span 180
        kw (ca id iq dcl opt kw mode dpr dct dpt span)
        fa (get_real 6 "Outer radius" 5.0)
        fb (get_real 6 "Left inner radius" 2.0)
        fc (- fa fb)
        eq_display (strcat "\nOuter radius = " (rtos fa 2 16) ", left inner radius = " (rtos fb 2 16) ", right inner radius = " (rtos fc 2 16))
        sr T
        a1 0
        a2 0
        d3 3
        xc '(cond ((= d3 3) (* fa (cos (eval a2)))) ((= d3 2) (+ (* fb (cos (eval a2))) (- fa) fb)) (T (- fa fc (- (* fc (cos (- pi (eval a2))))))))
        yc '(cond ((= d3 3) (* fa (sin (eval a2)))) ((= d3 2) (* fb (sin (- (eval a2))))) (T (* fc (sin (eval a2)))))
        x1 (eval xc)
        y1 (eval yc)
        a2 '(+ a1 paramstep)
        x2 '(eval xc)
        y2 '(eval yc)
        e1 (ssadd))
  (prompt baseinfo) (prompt eq_display) (setvar "osmode" 0)
  (command "pline" (list (+ x0 (* x1 cosangle) (- (* y1 sinangle))) (+ y0 (* y1 cosangle) (* x1 sinangle))))
  (setq sn divcount)
  (repeat 3
    (repeat sn
      (setq a3 (eval a2) x3 (eval x2) y3 (eval y2) a1 a3 x1 x3 y1 y3)
      (command (list (+ x0 (* x3 cosangle) (- (* y3 sinangle))) (+ y0 (* y3 cosangle) (* x3 sinangle)))))
    (setq d3 (- d3 1) e1 (ssadd (entlast) e1)))
  (command "" "pedit" "l" "j" e1 "l" "" "d" "")
  (setvar "osmode" os) (setvar "orthomode" om) (setvar "snapmode" sm)
  (setvar "snapbase" sb) (setvar "snapang" sa) (setvar "cmdecho" cd) (princ))
