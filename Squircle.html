Squircle

Two incompatible equal-axis quartics that share the same name: the superellipse and the Fernández-Guasti quartic that interpolates from a circle to a square.

Squircle SuperEllipse Cartesian equation:
\[ x^{4} + y^{4} = a^{4} \]
Squircle SuperEllipse parametric equations:
\[ x = a\,\mathrm{sgn}(\cos t)\,|\cos t|^{1/2},\quad y = a\,\mathrm{sgn}(\sin t)\,|\sin t|^{1/2} \]
Squircle Guasti implicit equation:
\[ s^{2}\left(\dfrac{x^{2}}{k^{2}}\right)\left(\dfrac{y^{2}}{k^{2}}\right) - \left(\dfrac{x^{2}}{k^{2}} + \dfrac{y^{2}}{k^{2}}\right) + 1 = 0 \]
Squircle Guasti parametric equations:
\[ x = t,\quad y = k\sqrt{\dfrac{k^{2}-t^{2}}{k^{2}-s^{2}t^{2}}},\quad t\in[-k,k] \]

History and Applications

The term squircle refers to two incompatible curves. The first is a special case of the superellipse with \(a = b\) and exponent 4, given by \(x^{4} + y^{4} = a^{4}\). Details of this form and its properties are provided in the Superellipse entry.1 The second definition, commonly called the Fernández-Guasti squircle, was introduced by Mexican physicist Manuel Fernández-Guasti in his paper on the analytic geometry of certain rectilinear figures.2 This quartic algebraic curve provides a continuous transition from a circle when the squareness parameter \(s = 0\) to a square when \(s = 1\). Fernández-Guasti and colleagues later used the name squircle in a 2005 paper examining LCD pixel geometry and the resulting far-field diffraction patterns.3

Real-life applications of the Fernández-Guasti squircle appear in optics. It models the geometry of LCD pixels and the far-field diffraction patterns produced by square or near-square apertures, improving accuracy over pure circle or square models in the design and analysis of optical systems and displays.3

(defun C:SQUIRCLE (/ basept xaxisangle cd sa sb sm om os divcount cosangle sinangle x0 y0 plineents segcount baseinfo paramstep
                     divpt xspan restartflag id iq dcl opt kw mode dpr dct dpt span eq_display xc yc skm a1 a2 x1 y1 x2 y2 fa fb)
  (setq id "Squircle"
        iq ""
        dcl "SQUIRCLE"
        opt '("Superellipse version" "Guasti version")
        kw 0
        mode 'param
        dpr "# of divisions"
        dct 360
        dpt '(1.0 0.0)
        span nil
        kw (ca id iq dcl opt kw mode dpr dct dpt span))
  (if (= kw 0)
    (progn
      (princ "\nSuperellipse version: x^4 + y^4 = a^4")
      (setq fa (get_real 2 "a (scale)" 1.0)
            eq_display (strcat "\na=" (rtos fa 2 4))
            xc '(* fa (if (< (cos (eval a2)) 0.0) -1.0 1.0) (alt_expt (abs (cos (eval a2))) 0.5 nil))
            yc '(* fa (if (< (sin (eval a2)) 0.0) -1.0 1.0) (alt_expt (abs (sin (eval a2))) 0.5 nil))
            paramstep (/ (* 2.0 pi) divcount)
            a1 0.0))
    (progn
      (princ "\nGuasti version: s²(x²/k²)(y²/k²)-((x²/k²)+(y²/k²))+1=0")
      (setq fa (get_real 2 "k (scale)" 1.0)
            fb (get_boundr 4 "s (squareness, 0=circle, 1=square)" 0.5 0.0 1.0 T nil)
            eq_display (strcat "\nk=" (rtos fa 2 4) ", s=" (rtos fb 2 4))
            xc '(eval a2)
            yc '(* fa (if (< (abs (- (* fa fa) (* fb fb (* (eval a2) (eval a2))))) 1e-8) 1.0 (alt_expt (/ (- (* fa fa) (* (eval a2) (eval a2)))
                (avoid_zero (- (* fa fa) (* fb fb (* (eval a2) (eval a2)))))) 0.5 nil)))
            a1 (- fa)
            paramstep (/ (* 2.0 fa) divcount))))
  (setq skm (if (eq kw 1) (if (> fb 0.99) '(T nil T T) '(T nil T)) '(T T T))
        a2 a1
        x1 (eval xc)
        y1 (eval yc)
        a2 '(+ a1 paramstep)
        x2 '(eval xc)
        y2 '(eval yc))
  (cc))
