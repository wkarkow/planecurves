Conchoid of Nicomedes

A classic plane algebraic curve invented by Nicomedes circa 200 BC for geometric constructions such as angle trisection and cube duplication.

Conchoid of Nicomedes polar equation:
\[ r = \frac{a}{\cos(\theta)} + b \]
Generalized polar equation:
\[ r = \frac{a}{\cos^{b}(c \theta)} + d \]

History and Applications

The conchoid of Nicomedes1,2,3, named after the Greek mathematician Nicomedes (second century BC), takes its name from the Greek konchoeidēs meaning “shell-like” because of its visual resemblance to a conch shell. Nicomedes developed the curve around 200 BC as a mechanical construction tool for neusis problems that cannot be solved with straightedge and compass alone. He used it specifically to trisect an arbitrary angle and to duplicate the cube (the Delian problem). Nicomedes identified three distinct curve forms depending on the relative sizes of the parameters. The curve was a favorite of seventeenth-century mathematicians; Isaac Newton suggested it be treated as one of the standard curves for analytical geometry. In Arithmetica Universalis, Newton recommended the conchoid of Nicomedes as an auxiliary curve in geometry because of the ease of its construction (it is sufficient to solve any problem involving equations of the third and fourth degrees). In the appendix on the linear construction of equations he makes extensive use of the “lemma of Nicomedes.4”

Real-life applications in engineering and the natural sciences are limited beyond these classical geometric constructions. The primary documented uses remain the ancient problems of angle trisection and cube duplication. No widespread modern applications in fields such as optics, acoustics, or structural mechanics were identified across the primary mathematical references; one isolated source describes an experimental heliostat design in solar power engineering that follows the conchoid path for mirror tracking.5

(defun C:CONCHNICOMEDES (/ basept xaxisangle cd sa sb sm om os divcount cosangle sinangle x0 y0 plineents segcount baseinfo paramstep
                         restartflag id iq dcl opt kw mode dpr dct dpt span eq_display xc yc skm a1 a2 x1 y1 x2 y2 fa fb fc fd)
  (setq id "Conchoid of Nicomedes"
        iq "r=a/cos^b(cθ)+d"
        dcl nil
        opt nil
        kw nil
        mode 'polar
        dpr "# of divisions in curve"
        dct 360
        dpt nil
        span 360
        kw (ca id iq dcl opt kw mode dpr dct dpt span)
        fa (get_real 2 "a (numerator scale)" 1.0)
        fb (get_real 0 "b (cosine exponent)" 1.0)
        fc (get_real 0 "c (angle multiplier)" 1.0)
        fd (get_real 0 "d (offset)" 1.0)
        eq_display (strcat "\na=" (rtos fa 2 4) ", b=" (rtos fb 2 4) ", c=" (rtos fc 2 4) ", d=" (rtos fd 2 4))
        rval '(+ (/ fa (avoid_zero (alt_expt (cos (* fc (eval a2))) fb nil))) fd)
        xc '(* (eval rval) (cos (eval a2)))
        yc '(* (eval rval) (sin (eval a2)))
        skm '(T T T)
        a1 0
        a2 a1
        x1 (eval xc)
        y1 (eval yc)
        a2 '(+ a1 paramstep)
        x2 '(eval xc)
        y2 '(eval yc))
  (cc))
