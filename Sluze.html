Sluze Cubic

A right rational circular cubic equation whose polar form \(r=a/\cos(n\theta)+(b^{2}/a)\cos(n\theta)\) has vertical asymptotes where \(\cos(n\theta)=0\).

Sluze Cubic implicit equation:
\[ a(x-a)(x^{2}+y^{2})=b^{2}x^{2} \]
Sluze Cubic polar equation:
\[ r = \dfrac{a}{\cos(\theta)} + \left(\dfrac{b^{2}}{a}\right)\cos(\theta) \]
Generalized polar equation:
\[ r = \dfrac{a}{\cos(n\theta)} ± \left(\frac{b^{2}}{a}\right)\cos(n\theta) \]

History and Applications

The Sluze cubic1,2,3,4, also called the conchoid of de Sluze or cubic of Sluze, was constructed in 1662 by René François Walter, baron de Sluze (1622–1685). De Sluze was a Walloon (Belgian) cleric who served in the church while pursuing mathematics; he is known for correspondence with Blaise Pascal and Christiaan Huygens and for his work on higher plane curves during the mid-17th century. The curve belongs to the family of right rational circular cubics with an isolated point and appears in classifications of cubic curves alongside the cissoid of Diocles and strophoids. The generalized equation will create a cusp when choosing Minus and setting \(b=a\) and a loop when choosing Minus and setting \(b>a\).

No real-life applications in engineering or the natural sciences were found.

  (setq id "Sluze Cubic"
        iq "r=a/cos(nθ)±(b²/a)·cos(nθ)"
        dcl nil
        opt nil
        kw nil
        mode 'polar
        dpr "# of divisions in curve"
        dct 360
        dpt nil
        span 180
        kw (ca id iq dcl opt kw mode dpr dct dpt span)
        fa (get_real 2 "a (scale & vertical asymptote position)" 2.0)
        fb (get_real 2 "b (strength of cubic feature)" 2.0)
        fc (get_real 2 "n (θ multiplier)" 1.0)
        fd (get_keyword 0 "Plus Minus" "\nPlus or Minus second term?" "Plus")
        fe (if (eq fd "Plus") 1.0 -1.0)
        eq_display (strcat "\na=" (rtos fa 2 4) ", b=" (rtos fb 2 4) ", n=" (rtos fc 2 4) ", " fd)
        ff 1.0
        rval '(progn (setq fg (cos (* fc (eval a2)))) (setq restartflag (and ff (< (* ff fg) 0))) (setq ff fg) (if restartflag 1e7
              (+ (/ fa (avoid_zero (cos (* fc (eval a2))))) (* fe (/ (* fb fb) fa) (cos (* fc (eval a2)))))))
        xc '(* (eval rval) (cos (eval a2)))
        yc '(* (eval rval) (sin (eval a2)))
        maxdraw (get_real 2 "Maximum x and y values allowed?" 50.0)
        skm '(T T T)
        a1 0
        a2 a1
        x1 (eval xc)
        y1 (eval yc)
        a2 '(+ a1 paramstep)
        x2 '(eval xc)
        y2 '(eval yc))
  (cc))
