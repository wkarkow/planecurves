Poinsot Spiral

Polar spirals corresponding to orbits under an inverse-cube central force.

Poinsot Spiral Curve polar equation:
\[ r = \dfrac{a}{\alpha\cdot\cosh(j\theta)+\beta\cdot\sinh(k\theta)} \]
Canonical Poinsot cosh polar equation:
\[ r = \dfrac{a}{\cosh(k\theta)} \]
Canonical Poinsot sinh polar equation:
\[ r = \dfrac{a}{\sinh(k\theta)} \]
Logarithmic spiral limit polar equation:
\[ r = \dfrac{a\cdot e^{\pm k\theta}}{\alpha} \]

History and Applications

The Poinsot spiral, also known as Cotes' spiral, was determined by Roger Cotes in 1722. It is named after French mathematician Louis Poinsot (1777–1859). The curve and its generalizations solve the central-force problem for a particle moving under an attractive or repulsive force directed toward the origin and proportional to the inverse cube of the distance. Binet's formula from orbital mechanics reduces this force law to the following polar equations.1,2 The two canonical cases are \( r = a/\cosh(k\theta) \) and \( r = a/\sinh(k\theta) \). In the combined, generalized equation, when \( \alpha = \pm\beta \) and \( j = k \) the equation reduces to the logarithmic spiral \( r = a\cdot e^{\mp k\theta}/\alpha \). The bounded form is the orthogonal projection onto the equatorial plane of a rhumb line (loxodrome) traced on a sphere.1

In the natural sciences the Poinsot family models trajectories of a particle under an inverse-cube central force. While gravity and electrostatic forces follow the inverse-square law and produce conic sections, the inverse-cube law produces these spirals. The spirals also arise as the inverse of the path traced by a marble rolling inside a uniformly rotating tube.1 No additional engineering or industrial applications beyond the classical-mechanics context were identified in the primary references.

(defun C:POINSOT (/ basept xaxisangle cd sa sb sm om os divcount cosangle sinangle x0 y0 plineents segcount baseinfo paramstep divpt xspan
                    restartflag id iq dcl opt kw mode dpr dct dpt span eq_display xc yc skm a1 a2 x1 y1 x2 y2 maxdraw fa fb fc fd fe ff)
  (setq id "Poinsot Spiral"
        iq "r=a/(α·cosh(jθ)+β·sinh(kθ))"
        dcl "POINSOT"
        opt '("Canonical Poinsot cosh" "Canonical Poinsot sinh" "Logarithmic Spiral" "Generalized Poinsot")
        kw 0
        mode 'polar
        dpr "# of divisions around circle"
        dct 360
        dpt nil
        span 360
        kw (ca id iq dcl opt kw mode dpr dct dpt span))
  (cond
   ((eq kw 0)
      (princ "\nPoinsot Spiral: r=a/cosh(kθ)")
      (setq fa (get_real 2 "a (scale)" 1.0)
            fb (get_real 2 "k (θ multiplier)" 0.2096003591394913)
            fc 1.0
            fd 0.0
            fe fb
            ff fb
            eq_display (strcat "\na=" (rtos fa 2 4) ", k=" (rtos fb 2 4))))
   ((eq kw 1)
      (princ "\nPoinsot Spiral: r=a/sinh(kθ)")
      (setq fa (get_real 2 "a (scale)" 1.0)
            fb (get_real 2 "k (θ multiplier)" 0.2096003591394913)
            fc 0.0
            fd 1.0
            fe fb
            ff fb
            eq_display (strcat "\na=" (rtos fa 2 4) ", k=" (rtos fb 2 4))))
   ((eq kw 2)
      (princ "\nPoinsot Limit (Logarithmic): r=a/(α·cosh(kθ)+β·sinh(kθ)), α=±β")
      (setq fa (get_real 2 "a (scale)" 1.0)
            fb (get_real 2 "k (theta multiplier)" 0.1)
            fc (get_real 2 "α (nonzero)" 1.0)
            fd (get_keyword 0 "Plus Minus" "β = Plus or Minus α?" "Plus")
            fe fb
            ff fb
            fd (if (eq fd "Minus") (- fc) fc)
            eq_display (strcat "\na=" (rtos fa 2 4) ", α=" (rtos fc 2 4) ", k=" (rtos fb 2 4) ", β=" (rtos fd 2 4))))
   ((eq kw 3)
      (princ "\nGeneralized Poinsot: r=a/(α·cosh(jθ)+β·sinh(kθ))")
      (setq fa (get_real 2 "a (scale)" 1.0)
            fc (get_real 2 "α (cosh weight)" 1.0)
            fd (get_real 2 "β (sinh weight)" -1.25)
            fe (get_real 2 "j (cosh rate)" 0.1)
            ff (get_real 2 "k (sinh rate)" 0.2)
            maxdraw (get_real 2 "Maximum x and y values allowed?" 50)
            eq_display (strcat "\na=" (rtos fa 2 4) ", α=" (rtos fc 2 4) ", β=" (rtos fd 2 4) ", j=" (rtos fe 2 4) ", k=" (rtos ff 2 4)))))
  (setq rval '(/ fa (avoid_zero (+ (* fc (* 0.5 (+ (exp (* fe (eval a2))) (exp (- (* fe (eval a2))))))) (* fd (* 0.5 (- (exp (* ff (eval a2))) (exp (- (* ff (eval a2))))))))))
        xc '(* (eval rval) (cos (eval a2)))
        yc '(* (eval rval) (sin (eval a2)))
        skm (if (eq kw 1) '(nil T T) (if (eq kw 3) '(nil nil nil) '(T nil T)))
        a1 0
        a2 a1
        x1 (eval xc)
        y1 (eval yc)
        a2 '(+ a1 paramstep)
        x2 '(eval xc)
        y2 '(eval yc))
  (cc))
(C:POINSOT)
