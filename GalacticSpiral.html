Galactic Spiral

A kinematic spiral generated by the ROTASE (Rotating Two-Arm Sprinkler Emission) model; morphology controlled by the single parameter ρ.

Galactic Spiral parent system:
\[ \frac{dx}{d\theta}=R\cdot\frac{y}{r},\qquad \frac{dy}{d\theta}=R\cdot\Bigl(\rho-\frac{x}{r}\Bigr) \]
Polar form (all ρ):
\[ r=\frac{R}{1-\rho\sin\alpha} \]
θ for ρ<1:
\[ \theta=\frac1R\Biggl[R\arcsin s-R\arcsin\Biggl(\frac{(1-\rho^2)R-R}{\rho R}\Biggr)-\frac{R\rho}{1-\rho^2}\Biggr],\quad s=\frac{(1-\rho^2)r-R}{\rho R} \]
θ for ρ=1:
\[ \theta=\frac{\sqrt2}{3\sqrt R}(r+R)\sqrt{r-\frac R2-\frac23 R} \]
θ for ρ>1:
\[ \theta=\frac1R\Biggl[\frac1{\sqrt{\rho^2-1}}\Biggl(\sqrt{(\rho^2-1)r^2+2rR-R^2}-\frac R{\sqrt{\rho^2-1}}\ln\Biggl|\frac{r\sqrt{\rho^2-1}+R}R\Biggr|\Biggr)-\frac R{\rho^2-1}\Biggl(\rho-\frac1{\sqrt{\rho^2-1}}\ln\Biggl|\frac{\rho^2 R+\rho R}{\sqrt{\rho^2-1}\,R}\Biggr|\Biggr)\Biggr] \]

History and Applications

The galactic spiral equations1,2 arise from a kinematic model proposed by Hongjun Pan in a series of papers beginning in 2019. Pan named the construction ROTASE (Rotating Two-Arm Sprinkler Emission). In the model a rigid bar of half-length \(R\) rotates at constant angular speed while material is emitted from each end at a speed \(V_e\). The surrounding disc is assumed to possess a flat rotation curve of speed \(V_r\). The single free parameter is the ratio \(\rho=V_e/V_r\). Differentiating the resulting velocity field with respect to the bar angle \(\theta\) (used as a time parameter) produces the parent system shown below, with initial condition \((x,y)=(0,R)\). After integration every point is rotated backward by \(-\theta\).

Closed-form integration in polar coordinates yields three regimes controlled by the constant value of \(\rho\): a spiral-ring of outer radius \(R/(1-\rho)\) when \(\rho<1\), an unbounded Fermat-like spiral when \(\rho=1\), and an open spiral that approaches the Archimedean form for large \(\rho>1\). Mild time dependence \(\rho(\theta)\) generates additional morphologies such as double rings.

The construction is purely kinematic. It does not follow from the collisionless Boltzmann equation, the Poisson equation, or the Lin–Shu density-wave relation, nor does it recover those theories as special cases. Two papers2,3 demonstrate that suitable choices of \(\rho\) can reproduce the gross appearance of many observed spiral and ring galaxies and, with the same equations, certain plant phyllotactic patterns. Those demonstrations remain geometric fittings. Standard dynamical accounts of spiral structure (density waves, swing amplification, manifold theory) are not obtained from the model.

No engineering applications of the specific differential system or its closed-form solutions have been reported. The equations function as a compact morphological generator for spiral-like shapes; they do not encode the physical mechanisms presently regarded as responsible for galactic spiral arms.
