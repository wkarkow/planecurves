Ehrhart Egg

A closed convex curve (triellipse) defined as the locus of points whose summed distances to three fixed collinear foci equal a constant k approximating a hen’s egg.

Ehrhart Egg implicit polar equation:
\[ r + \sqrt{r^{2}-2\cdot b\cdot r\cdot\sin(\theta)+b^{2}} + \sqrt{r^{2}-2\cdot c\cdot r\cdot\sin(\theta)+c^{2}} = k \]

History and Applications

The Ehrhart egg1, also known as a triellipse or three-foci hyperellipse, is a closed convex curve defined as the locus of points where the sum of distances to three fixed foci equals a constant value k. French mathematician Eugène Ehrhart (1906–2000), a high school teacher who conducted independent research in geometry and combinatorics, introduced this specific curve in 1957. Ehrhart chose collinear foci and parameters that approximate the averaged shape of a real hen’s egg based on statistical measurements. The broader family of multifocal ellipses (n-ellipses or polyellipses), of which the triellipse is a special case, traces back to Ehrenfried Walther von Tschirnhaus in 1686 and to a mechanical string-construction method published by James Clerk Maxwell in 18462.

In the natural sciences the Ehrhart egg serves as a practical mathematical model for avian egg morphology. Biologists have used it and closely related triellipses to quantify and compare egg shapes across bird species, aiding studies in ornithology, developmental biology, and evolutionary morphology. No documented applications in engineering fields appear in the primary curve-reference sources.

(defun C:EHRHART (/ basept xaxisangle cd sa sb sm om os divcount cosangle sinangle x0 y0 plineents segcount baseinfo paramstep divpt restartflag
                    id iq dcl opt kw mode dpr dct dpt span eq_display xc yc skm a1 a2 x1 y1 x2 y2 fa fb fc fd fe ff fg fh fi fj fk fl fm fn fo)
  (setq id "Ehrhart Egg"
        iq "r + √(r²-2·b·r·sin(θ)+b²)+√(r²-2·c·r·sin(θ)+c²)=k"
        dcl nil
        opt nil
        kw nil
        mode 'polar
        dpr "# of divisions in curve"
        dct 360
        dpt nil
        span 360
        kw (ca id iq dcl opt kw mode dpr dct dpt span)
        fa (get_real 0 "b (B focus at (0,b))" 0.2)
        fb (get_real 6 "c (C focus at (0,c))" 1.0)
        fc (get_real 6 "k (sum distance constant)" 2.2)
        eq_display (strcat "\nb=" (rtos fa 2 4) ", c=" (rtos fb 2 4) ", k=" (rtos fc 2 4))
        xc '(progn
              (setq fd (eval a2)
                    fe (cos fd)
                    ff (sin fd)
                    fg fa
                    fh fb
                    fi fc
                    fj 0.0
                    fk (avoid_zero fi)
                    fl 0)
              (while (and (< fl 24) (< (+ fk (sqrt (max 0.0 (+ (* fk fk) (* -2.0 fk fg ff) (* fg fg)))) (sqrt (max 0.0 (+ (* fk fk) (* -2.0 fk fh ff) (* fh fh))))) fi))
                (setq fk (* 2.0 fk)
                      fl (1+ fl)))
              (setq fl 0)
              (while (< fl 48) 
                (setq fm (* 0.5 (+ fj fk))
                      fn (- (+ fm (sqrt (max 0.0 (+ (* fm fm) (* -2.0 fm fg ff) (* fg fg)))) (sqrt (max 0.0 (+ (* fm fm) (* -2.0 fm fh ff) (* fh fh))))) fi))
                (if (<= fn 0.0)
                  (setq fj fm)
                  (setq fk fm))
                (setq fl (1+ fl)))
              (setq fo fj)
              (* fo fe))
        yc '(* fo ff)
        skm '(T T T)
        a1 0
        a2 a1
        x1 (eval xc)
        y1 (eval yc)
        a2 '(+ a1 paramstep)
        x2 '(eval xc)
        y2 '(eval yc))
  (cc))
