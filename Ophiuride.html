Ophiuride

A rational circular cubic with a double point at the origin.

Ophiuride Curve polar equation:
\[ r = (b\sin\theta - a\cos\theta)\tan\theta \]
Generalized polar equation:
\[ r = \bigl(b\sin(m\theta) - a\cos(n\theta)\bigr)\tan(o\theta) \]

History and Applications

The Ophiuride curve1,2,3 was studied by Diedrich Uhlhorn in 1809. Its name derives from the Greek words ophis (“snake”) and oura (“tail”), reflecting its resemblance to the sinuous form of a brittle star (ophiuroid), an echinoderm related to the starfish. Like all rational circular cubics, the ophiuride admits multiple geometric constructions and is characterized by a double point at the origin. When the cosine amplitude parameter vanishes it reduces to the cissoid of Diocles. Curves of this class were typical of 18th- and 19th-century work that translated angle-based geometric loci into polar equations and then into implicit Cartesian forms, enabling classification by algebraic degree and singularities.

The ophiuride has no known direct applications in engineering or the natural sciences.

AutoLISP Code

(defun C:OPHIURIDE (/ basept xaxisangle cd sa sb sm om os divcount cosangle sinangle x0 y0 plineents segcount baseinfo paramstep divpt xspan
                      restartflag id iq dcl opt kw mode dpr dct dpt span eq_display xc yc skm a1 a2 x1 y1 x2 y2 maxdraw fa fb fc fd fe rval)
  (setq id "Ophiuride Curve"
        iq ""
        dcl "OPHIURIDE"
        opt '("Canonical" "Generalized")
        kw 0
        mode 'polar
        dpr "# of divisions around circle"
        dct 360
        dpt nil
        span 180
        kw (ca id iq dcl opt kw mode dpr dct dpt span))
  (cond
   ((eq kw 0)
    (princ "\nOphiuride: r=(b·sin(θ)-a·cos(θ))·tan(θ) ")
    (setq fa (get_real 6 "b (sine amplitude)" 1.0)
          fc (get_real 4 "a (cosine amplitude)" 1.0)
          fb 1.0
          fd 1.0
          fe 1.0
          eq_display (strcat "b=" (rtos fb 2 4) ", a=" (rtos fc 2 4))))
   ((eq kw 1)
    (princ "\nGeneralized ophiuride: r=(b·sin(mθ)-a·cos(nθ))·tan(oθ)")
    (setq fa (get_real 6 "b (sine amplitude)" 1.0)
          fb (get_real 6 "m (sine multiplier)" 1.0)
          fc (get_real 4 "a (cosine amplitude)" 1.0)
          fd (get_real 6 "n (cosine multiplier)" 1.0)
          fe (get_real 6 "o (tangent multiplier)" 1.0)
          eq_display (strcat "b=" (rtos fa 2 4) ", m=" (rtos fb 2 4) ", a=" (rtos fc 2 4) ", n=" (rtos fd 2 4) ", o=" (rtos fe 2 4)))))
  (setq rval '(* (- (* fa (sin (* fb (eval a2)))) (* fc (cos (* fd (eval a2))))) (tan (* fe (eval a2))))
        xc '(* (eval rval ) (cos (eval a2)))
        yc '(* (eval rval ) (sin (eval a2)))
        maxdraw (get_real 2 "Maximum x and y values allowed?" 1e5)
        skm '(T T T)
        a1 (/ pi -2.0)
        a2 a1
        x1 (eval xc)
        y1 (eval yc)
        a2 '(+ a1 paramstep)
        x2 '(eval xc)
        y2 '(eval yc))
  (cc))
