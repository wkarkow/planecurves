Bowtie Curve

Decorative polar curve producing a characteristic bowtie or winged shape with asymptotic rays.

Bowtie Curve polar equation (classical):
\[ r = \frac{\cos(2\theta)}{\cos^{2}(\theta)} \]
Generalized Bowtie Curve polar equation:
\[ r = s \cdot \frac{\cos^{a}(b\theta)}{\cos^{c}(d\theta)} \]

History and Applications

The Bowtie curve, in its classical form \( r = \frac{\cos(2\theta)}{\cos^{2}(\theta)} \), is catalogued among decorative analytic curves and does not originate from a geometric locus or mechanical linkage.1 Using the identity \(\cos(2\theta) = 2\cos^{2}\theta - 1\), it may be rewritten \( r = 1 - \tan^{2}\theta \), revealing it as a rational function of \(\tan(\theta)\). Its characteristic "wings" arise from polar directions where \(\cos(\theta) = 0\), producing asymptotic rays. For this reason, sources such as MathCurve classify this idea into two-frequency harmonic interaction.1,2

When the denominator exponent c > 0, zeros in the denominator create asymptotic structures; when c < 0, the expression becomes a product of cosine powers and typically yields closed figures.

Although the Bowtie itself has no traditional engineering application, products of cosine functions with independent frequencies and powers appear naturally in signal modulation, antenna and acoustic radiation patterns, optical interference models, and procedural geometric design.3

AutoLISP Code

(defun C:BOWTIE (/ basept xaxisangle cd sa sb sm om os divcount cosangle sinangle x0 y0 plineents segcount baseinfo paramstep
                   divpt xspan restartflag id iq dcl opt kw mode dpr dct dpt span eq_display xc yc skm a1 a2 x1 y1 x2 y2 fa fb fc fd fe)
  (setq id "Bowtie Curve"
        iq "r=s·cos^a(bθ)/cos^c(dθ)"
        dcl nil
        opt nil
        kw nil
        mode 'polar
        dpr "# of divisions around circle"
        dct 360
        dpt nil
        span 360
        kw (ca id iq dcl opt kw mode dpr dct dpt span)
        fa (get_real 2 "s (scale)" 1.0)
        fb (get_real 2 "a (cos numerator power)" 1.0)
        fc (get_real 2 "b (numerator θ multiple)" 2.0)
        fd (get_real 2 "c (cos denominator power)" 2.0)
        fe (get_real 2 "d (denominator θ multiple)" 1.0)
        eq_display (strcat "\ns=" (rtos fa 2 4) ", a=" (rtos fb 2 4) ", b=" (rtos fc 2 4) ", c=" (rtos fd 2 4) ", d=" (rtos fe 2 4))
        rval '(/ (* fa (alt_expt (cos (* fc (eval a2))) fb nil)) (avoid_zero (alt_expt (cos (* fe (eval a2))) fd nil)))
        xc '(* (eval rval) (cos (eval a2)))
        yc '(* (eval rval) (sin (eval a2)))
        skm '(T T T)
        a1 0
        a2 a1
        x1 (eval xc)
        y1 (eval yc)
        a2 '(+ a1 paramstep)
        x2 '(eval xc)
        y2 '(eval yc))
  (cc))
(C:BOWTIE)
