Reciprocal Exponential Curve

The transformed exponential \( y = a \cdot e^{b/x} \) has a vertical asymptote at \( x = 0 \) and a horizontal asymptote at \( y = a \).

Reciprocal Exponential Curve Cartesian equation:
\[ y = a \cdot e^{b/x} \]

History and Applications

The reciprocal exponential curve1, of the form \( y = a \cdot e^{b/x} \), is a transformed exponential function that exhibits a vertical asymptote at \( x = 0 \) and a horizontal asymptote at \( y = a \) (for positive \( a \) and \( b \)). For negative \( x \) the function approaches 0. It does not have classical origins but functions as a modern empirical model that arises naturally in calculus teaching as an example of one-sided limits, chain-rule differentiation, and asymptotic behavior.

In the natural sciences it appears in quantum mechanics. For a system of harmonic oscillators the proportion of population in quantum state \( n + 1 \) relative to state \( n \), plotted against temperature, follows this curve.2 In engineering it serves as the reciprocal exponential cost–tolerance function, capturing the nonlinear rise in manufacturing cost as dimensional tolerances tighten; this model supports statistical tolerance allocation that minimizes total production cost while meeting design specifications for multi-part assemblies.3 It has also been applied as an approximation to indentation load–depth curves during materials characterization4 and to regression modeling of shear-strength properties in clay and silt embankment soils within geotechnical engineering.5

AutoLISP Code

(defun C:RECIPEXP (/ basept xaxisangle cd sa sb sm om os divcount cosangle sinangle x0 y0 plineents segcount baseinfo paramstep
                   divpt restartflag id iq dcl opt kw mode dpr dct dpt span eq_display xc yc a1 a2 x1 y1 x2 y2 fa fb)
  (setq id "Reciprocal Exponential Curve"
        iq "y=a·e^{b/x}"
        dcl nil
        opt nil
        kw nil
        mode 'param
        dpr "# of divisions to x-span of division point"
        dct 500
        dpt '(5.0 0.0)
        span nil
        kw (ca id iq dcl opt kw mode dpr dct dpt span)
        fa (get_real 2 "a (y-scale multiplier)" 1.0)
        fb (get_real 2 "b (exponent numerator)" 1.0)
        fc (if (eq (get_keyword 0 "Positive Negative" "Positive or Negative x?" "Positive") "Positive") T nil)
        eq_display (strcat "\na=" (rtos fa 2 4) ", b=" (rtos fb 2 4))
        xc '(eval a2)
        yc '(* fa (exp (/ fb (avoid_zero (eval a2)))))
        paramstep (/ xspan divcount)
        paramstep (if fc paramstep (- paramstep))
        a1 0
        a2 a1
        x1 (eval xc)
        y1 (eval yc)
        a2 '(+ a1 paramstep)
        x2 '(eval xc)
        y2 '(eval yc))
  (cc))
