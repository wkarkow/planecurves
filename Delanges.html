Delanges Sectrix

A rational circular quartic; a trisectrix that is the polar inverse of Dürer’s folium.

Delanges Trisectrix implicit equation:
\[ (x^{2} + y^{2} - 2a^{2})^{2} = x^{2}(x^{2} + y^{2}) \]
Delanges Trisectrix polar equation:
\[ r = \dfrac{a}{\cos(\theta/2)} \quad \text{or} \quad r = a \sec(\theta/2) \]
Delanges Sectrix polar equation:
\[ r = \dfrac{a}{\cos(\theta/n)} \quad \text{or} \quad r = a \sec(\theta/n) \]

History and Applications

The Delanges trisectrix1, also known as the Delanges sectrix1, was studied by the Italian engineer and mathematician Paolo Delanges (ca. 1750–1810). He presented it in his 1783 publication La Trisegante, nuova curva e pensieri sulla Formula Cardanica2. The curve is a rational circular quartic that belongs to the family of sectrix curves, which solve the graphical problem of dividing an arbitrary angle into any integer number of equal parts. The Delanges form specifically acts as a trisectrix1. The sectrix for a given n is an (n+1)-sectrix.

It is the polar inverse (with respect to the origin) of Dürer’s folium1. The curve was rediscovered in 1878 by the American mathematician Hillhouse, who constructed a mechanical tracer to draw it2. A geometric construction begins with a circle of radius 2a centered at the origin and a fixed line through the origin; the locus of point M satisfies the trisection property that the angle at the origin formed by M is one-third the angle formed by a related point on the circle1.

Real-life applications in engineering and the natural sciences are limited and historical. The curve supported the design of mathematical instruments and mechanical linkages for precise angle division in classical geometric constructions and drafting tools2. No major contemporary uses in fields such as optics, structural mechanics, or biological modeling are documented.

AutoLISP Code

(defun C:DELANGES (/ basept xaxisangle cd sa sb sm om os divcount cosangle sinangle x0 y0 plineents segcount baseinfo divpt xstep paramstep
                     restartflag id iq dcl opt kw mode dpr dct dpt span rval xc yc skm a1 a2 x1 y1 x2 y2 eq_display fa fb)
  (setq id "Delanges Sectrix"
        iq "r=a/cos(θ/n)"
        dcl nil
        opt nil
        kw nil
        mode 'polar
        dpr "# of divisions in curve"
        dct 360
        dpt nil
        span 360
        kw (ca id iq dcl opt kw mode dpr dct dpt span)
        fa (get_real 2 "a (scale)" 1.0)
        fb (get_real 2 "n (angle divisor, 2 for trisectrix)" 2.0)
        eq_display (strcat "\nr=" (rtos fa 2 4) ", n=" (rtos fb 2 4))
        rval '(/ fa (avoid_zero (cos (/ (eval a2) fb))))
        xc '(* (eval rval) (cos (eval a2)))
        yc '(* (eval rval) (sin (eval a2)))
        skm '(T nil T)
        a1 0
        a2 a1
        x1 (eval xc)
        y1 (eval yc)
        a2 '(+ a1 paramstep)
        x2 '(eval xc)
        y2 '(eval yc))
  (cc))
