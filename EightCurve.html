Eight Curve

A quartic algebraic curve that crosses itself at the origin to form a figure-eight or infinity-symbol path.

Eight Curve implicit equation:
\[ x^{4} = a^{2}(x^{2} - y^{2}) \]
Eight Curve parametric equations:
\[ x = a \cdot \sin(\theta),\quad y = a \cdot \sin(\theta) \cdot \cos(\theta) \]
Eight Curve polar equation:
\[ r^{2} = a^{2} \cdot \sec^{4}(\theta)\cos(2\theta) \]
Generalized parametric equations:
\[ x = a \cdot \sin^{b}(\theta),\quad y = a \cdot \sin^{c}(\theta)\cos^{d}(\theta) \]

History and Applications

The Eight Curve1,2, also known as the lemniscate of Gerono or figure-eight curve, is a quartic algebraic curve that crosses itself at the origin to form a path resembling the numeral 8 lying on its side or the infinity symbol ∞. The curve was studied by Grégoire de Saint-Vincent in 1647 and by Gabriel Cramer in 1750. It received its modern name from Aubry in 1895 in honor of the French mathematician Camille-Christophe Gerono (1799–1891). Gerono co-founded the Nouvelles Annales de Mathématiques, a prominent French mathematics journal intended for students and teachers, alongside Olry Terquem. The journal was notable for promoting accessible mathematics during a period of increasing specialization in the field. Leibniz referenced a related figure-eight curve in a 1691 letter to Huygens, giving rise to the occasional alternative name lemniscate of Huygens. The parametric form implemented in the AutoLISP code offers a generalized family of powered curves that includes the classic Eight Curve when the exponents b = c = d = 1. Its equation is similar to that of the dumbbell curve.

In engineering, linkage mechanisms have been synthesized to generate the curve for use in textile machinery, mechanical toys, and precision curve-drawing instruments.3 As a special 1:2 Lissajous curve, it also appears in vibration analysis and oscilloscope-based frequency/phase comparison.

AutoLISP Code

(defun C:EIGHTCURVE (/ basept xaxisangle cd sa sb sm om os divcount cosangle sinangle x0 y0 plineents segcount baseinfo paramstep divpt
                       restartflag id iq dcl opt kw mode dpr dct dpt span eq_display xc yc skm a1 a2 x1 y1 x2 y2 maxdraw fa fb fc fd)
  (setq id "Eight Curve"
        iq "x=a·sinᵇ(θ), y=a·sinᶜ(θ)·cosᵈ(θ)"
        dcl nil
        opt nil
        kw nil
        mode 'polar
        dpr "# of divisions in curve"
        dct 360
        dpt nil
        span 360
        kw (ca id iq dcl opt kw mode dpr dct dpt span)
        fa (get_real 2 "a (scale)" 1.0)
        fb (get_real 2 "b (x sin exponent)" 1.0)
        fc (get_real 2 "c (y sin exponent)" 1.0)
        fd (get_real 2 "d (y cos exponent)" 1.0)
        eq_display (strcat "\na=" (rtos fa 2 4) ", b=" (rtos fb 2 4) ", c=" (rtos fc 2 4) ", d=" (rtos fd 2 4))
        xc '(* fa (alt_expt (sin (eval a2)) fb nil))
        yc '(* fa (alt_expt (sin (eval a2)) fc nil) (alt_expt (cos (eval a2)) fd nil))
        maxdraw (if (or (< fb 0) (< fc 0) (< fd 0)) (get_real 2 "Maximum x and y values allowed?" 1e5) 1e5)
        skm '(T T T)
        a1 0
        a2 a1
        x1 (eval xc)
        y1 (eval yc)
        a2 '(+ a1 paramstep)
        x2 '(eval xc)
        y2 '(eval yc))
  (cc))
(C:EIGHTCURVE)
