Fish Curve

Two curves named for their visual resemblance to a fish silhouette; a classic single form and a Siamese form.

Single Fish Curve parametric equations:
\[ x = a(\cos(t) + 2k \cdot \cos(t/2)),\quad y = a \cdot \sin(t) \]
Siamese Fish Curve parametric equations:
\[ x = a\bigl(5\cos(t) - (\sqrt{2}-1)\cos(5t)\bigr),\quad y = a \cdot \sin(4t) \]

History and Applications

The Fish Curve is a quartic plane curve named for its visual resemblance to a fish silhouette. British mathematician E. H. Lockwood described the negative pedal curve of an ellipse taken with respect to one focus in his 1957 paper, establishing the mathematical basis for the classic form when the squared eccentricity equals 1/2 and produces the characteristic cusp and loop.1 A separate geometric construction for the general fish curve with fin factor k defines it as the locus of intersection points between a variable circle (centered at point N on a fixed base circle with radius k times distance ON) and a fixed straight line parallel to a reference diameter; this generalizes the strophoid and yields the Single Fish variant when k = √2.2 The curve is also the projection of Viviani’s space curve onto planes passing through the y-axis. The Siamese Fish is a specific harmonic variant within the same family.

No applications in engineering or the natural sciences are documented for these parametric forms.

References

  2. https://mathcurve.com/courbes2d.gb/poisson/poisson.shtml (k-parameter construction, Siamese variant, and homemade name).

AutoLISP Code

(defun C:FISH (/ basept xaxisangle cd sa sb sm om os divcount cosangle sinangle x0 y0 plineents segcount baseinfo paramstep divpt
                 xspan restartflag id iq dcl opt kw mode dpr dct dpt span eq_display xc yc skm a1 a2 x1 y1 x2 y2 fa fb)
  (setq id "Fish Curve"
        iq ""
        dcl "FISH"
        opt '("Single" "Siamese")
        kw 0
        mode 'polar
        dpr "# of divisions in curve"
        dct 360
        dpt nil
        span 360
        kw (ca id iq dcl opt kw mode dpr dct dpt span))
  (cond
   ((eq kw 0)
    (princ "\nSingle Fish: x=a·(cos(t)+2·k·cos(t/2)), y=a·sin(t)")
    (setq fa (get_real 2 "a (scale)" 1.0)
          fb (get_real 2 "k (fin factor)" (sqrt 2.0))
          eq_display (strcat "\na=" (rtos fa 2 4) ", k=" (rtos fb 2 4))
          paramstep (* paramstep 2.0)
          xc '(* fa (+ (cos (eval a2)) (* 2.0 fb (cos (* 0.5 (eval a2))))))
          yc '(* fa (sin (eval a2)))))
   ((eq kw 1)
    (princ "\nSiamese Fish: x=a·(5·cos(t)-(√2-1)·cos(5·t)), y=a·sin(4·t)")
    (setq fa (get_real 2 "a (scale)" 1.0)
          eq_display (strcat "\na=" (rtos fa 2 4))
          xc '(* fa (- (* 5.0 (cos (eval a2))) (* (- (sqrt 2.0) 1.0) (cos (* 5.0 (eval a2))))))
          yc '(* fa (sin (* 4.0 (eval a2)))))))
  (setq skm '(T T T)
        a1 0
        a2 a1
        x1 (eval xc)
        y1 (eval yc)
        a2 '(+ a1 paramstep)
        x2 '(eval xc)
        y2 '(eval yc))
  (cc)
  (command "pedit" "l" "d" "")
  (princ))
(C:FISH)
