Circular Asymptotic Spiral

A polar spiral that winds infinitely many times around a limiting circle of radius a, approaching it asymptotically from both sides.

e c
Circular Asymptotic Spiral polar equation:
\[ r = \dfrac{a \cdot t + b}{t - c} \]

History and Applications

The Circular Asymptotic Spiral has no established prior name in the curve literature; the descriptive label is used here for the polar family \( r = (a \cdot t + b)/(t - c) \) that approaches a circle of radius \( |a| \) centered at the origin as \( t \to \pm \infty \). The notion of a circular asymptote itself appears in nineteenth-century treatises on polar curves. Bartholomew Price, in his Treatise on Infinitesimal Calculus1, defines an asymptotic circle as the limiting circle of finite radius approached by a polar curve when the angle increases without bound, distinguishing interior and exterior cases according to whether the curve lies outside or inside the circle. Price gives elementary examples of the form \( r = a(1 \pm 1/\theta) \) that exhibit this behavior. Serge Mehl’s modern pedagogical page2 presents the concrete specimen \( r = (2t + 1)/(t - 1) \) to illustrate the phenomenon: as \( t \to \pm \infty \) the curve winds both externally and internally about the circle \( r = 2 \), while near the simple pole \( t = 1 \) a pair of parabolic branches appears. A French Wikipedia page3 shows the equation \(r=t/(t+1) \) and its image as a spiral asymptotic to a circle. No earlier attribution to a particular mathematician, nor any catalogue entry under a fixed name, has been located on MathCurve, MathWorld, or 2dcurves.

No documented real-life applications of the Circular Asymptotic Spiral in engineering or the natural sciences have been identified. The curve remains a textbook illustration of circular asymptotes in polar coordinates.

AutoLISP Code

(defun C:ASYMPCIRCLE (/ basept xaxisangle cd sa sb sm om os divcount cosangle sinangle x0 y0 plineents segcount baseinfo divpt xstep paramstep
                       restartflag id iq dcl opt kw mode dpr dct dpt span eq_display rval xc yc skm a1 a2 x1 y1 x2 y2 fa fb fc fd maxdraw)
  (setq id "Asymptotic Circle"
        iq "r=(a·t+b)/(t-c)"
        dcl nil
        opt nil
        kw 0
        mode 'polar
        dpr "# of divisions around circle"
        dct 360
        dpt nil
        span 360
        kw (ca id iq dcl opt kw mode dpr dct dpt span)
        kw 0
        fa (get_real 2 "a (asymptotic radius)" 1.0)
        fb (get_real 2 "b (pole along -x)" 1.0)
        fc (get_real 2 "c (pole divisor & spiral tightness)" 1.0)
        fd (get_keyword 0 "Plus Minus" "Plus or Minus branch?" "Plus")
        eq_display (strcat "\na=" (rtos fa 2 4) ", b=" (rtos fb 2 4) ", c=" (rtos fc 2 4))
        paramstep (if (eq fd "Plus") paramstep (- paramstep))
        rval '(/ (+ (* fa (eval a2)) fb) (avoid_zero (- (eval a2) fc)))
        xc '(* (eval rval) (cos (eval a2)))
        yc '(* (eval rval) (sin (eval a2)))
        skm '(T T T)
        maxdraw (get_real 2 "Maximum x and y values allowed?" 20.0)
        a1 0
        a2 a1
        x1 (eval xc)
        y1 (eval yc)
        a2 '(+ a1 paramstep)
        x2 '(eval xc)
        y2 '(eval yc))
  (cc))
