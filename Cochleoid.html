Cochleoid

A transcendental spiral curve (the "snail curve").

Cochleoid polar equation:
\[ r = \frac{a \sin \theta}{\theta} \]
Generalized Cochleoid polar equation:
\[ r = \frac{a \sin^{b} (c \theta)}{\theta^{d}} \]

History and Applications

The cochleoid,1,2,3 meaning “snail-form” in Latin from the Greek kokhlias (snail or shell), is a transcendental spiral curve. It was first considered by John Perks (referenced in Wallis et al. 1699) and studied by John Wallis in 1685, J. Peck in 1700, and Johann Bernoulli in 1726. Ernesto Cesàro examined it further in 1878, while Falkenburg in 1884 (with Bentham/Benthan) established the modern name. A particular parametric form is due to the Belgian mathematician Joseph Neuberg. The curve is the polar inverse (with respect to the origin and power a²) of the quadratrix of Hippias (Dinostratus). Geometrically, it is the locus of the free extremity of a circular arc of fixed length α whose other end is fixed and remains tangent to a straight line; equivalently, it is the barycentric curve of the circle (center of gravity of a uniform arc of fixed length α) and arises as a conical perspective of a circular helix viewed along its axis.

In engineering and the natural sciences, applications center on kinematics and mechanism synthesis.4 The curve has been used to analyze the motion of a material point traveling along the cochleoid, yielding exact velocity and acceleration equations useful for linkage design and curve-generation mechanisms. It also appears in studies of pure bending of sheets and in classical constructions for approximate rectification of circular arcs. No widespread uses beyond these geometric and kinematic contexts are documented.

AutoLISP Code

(defun C:COCHLEOID (/ basept xaxisangle cd sa sb sm om os divcount cosangle sinangle x0 y0 plineents segcount baseinfo paramstep
                    restartflag rval xc yc skm a1 a2 x1 y1 x2 y2 eq_display fa fb fc fd id iq dcl opt kw mode dpr dct dpt span)
  (setq id "Cochleoid"
        iq "r=a·sinᵇ(c·θ)/θᵈ"
        dcl "COCHLEOID"
        opt '("Cochleoid" "New Values")
        kw 0
        mode 'polar
        dpr "# of divisions around circle"
        dct 360
        dpt nil
        span 360
        kw (ca id iq dcl opt kw mode dpr dct dpt span))
  (cond
   ((= kw 0)
    (setq fa 1.0 fb 1.0 fc 1.0 fd 1.0))
   ((= kw 1)
    (setq fa (get_real 2 "a (scale)" 1.0)
          fb (get_real 2 "b (exponent of sin(c·θ)" 1.0)
          fc (get_real 2 "c (θ multiplier)" 1.0)
          fd (get_real 2 "d (exponent of denominator θ)" 1.0))))
  (setq eq_display (strcat "\na=" (rtos fa 2 4) ", b=" (rtos fb 2 4) ", c=" (rtos fc 2 4) ", d=" (rtos fd 2 4))
        rval '(if (< (abs (eval a2)) 1e-6) (cond ((= fb fd) (* fa (alt_expt fc fb nil))) ((> fb fd) 0.0) ((< fb fd) 1e6))
              (/ (* fa (alt_expt (sin (* fc (eval a2))) fb nil)) (avoid_zero (alt_expt (eval a2) fd nil))))
        xc '(* (eval rval) (cos (eval a2)))
        yc '(* (eval rval) (sin (eval a2)))
        skm '(T T T)
        a1 0
        a2 a1
        x1 (eval xc)
        y1 (eval yc)
        a2 '(+ a1 paramstep)
        x2 '(eval xc)
        y2 '(eval yc))
  (cc))
