Cayley's Sextic

A sextic algebraic curve belonging to the sinusoidal spiral family; the pedal of a cardioid taken at the cardioid cusp point.

Cayley's Sextic implicit equation:
\[ 4(x^{2} + y^{2} - a x)^{3} = 27 a^{2} (x^{2} + y^{2})^{2} \]
Cayley's Sextic classical polar equation:
\[ r = 4a \cos^{3}\left(\frac{\theta}{3}\right) \]
Generalized polar equation (as coded):
\[ r = a \cdot \cos^{b}(c \theta) \]

History and Applications

Cayley's sextic is a plane algebraic curve of degree six that belongs to the family of sinusoidal spirals. It was first discussed by the Scottish mathematician Colin Maclaurin in 1718.1 The British mathematician Arthur Cayley (1821–1895) later studied the curve in detail. The name “Cayley's sextic” was assigned by R. C. Archibald in a 1900 paper classifying plane curves.2 The curve is the pedal of a cardioid taken with respect to the cardioid's cusp point. It is also the polar inverse of the Tschirnhausen cubic with respect to its focus, and its evolute is a nephroid.3 It can be generated as a roulette when one cardioid rolls around another of equal size.

AutoLISP Code

(defun C:CAYLEYSEXTIC (/ basept xaxisangle cd sa sb sm om os divcount cosangle sinangle x0 y0 plineents segcount baseinfo paramstep
                         restartflag id iq dcl opt kw mode dpr dct dpt span eq_display xc yc skm a1 a2 x1 y1 x2 y2 fa fb fc rval)
  (setq id "Cayley's Sextic"
        iq "r=a·cos^b(c·θ)"
        dcl nil
        opt nil
        kw nil
        mode 'polar
        dpr "# of divisions around circle"
        dct 360
        dpt nil
        span 360
        kw (ca id iq dcl opt kw mode dpr dct dpt span)
        fa (get_real 2 "a (scale)" 1.0)
        fb (get_real 2 "b (cosine exponent)" 3.0)
        fc (get_real 2 "c (θ multiplier)" (/ 1.0 3.0))
        eq_display (strcat "\na=" (rtos fa 2 4) ", b=" (rtos fb 2 4) ", c=" (rtos fc 2 4))
        rval '(alt_expt (cos (* fc (eval a2))) fb T)
        xc '(* fa (eval rval) (cos (eval a2)))
        yc '(* fa (eval rval) (sin (eval a2)))
        skm '(nil T T)
        a1 0.0
        a2 a1
        x1 (eval xc)
        y1 (eval yc)
        a2 '(+ a1 paramstep)
        x2 '(eval xc)
        y2 '(eval yc))
  (cc))
(C:CAYLEYSEXTIC)
