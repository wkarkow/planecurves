Kulp Quartic

A quartic plane algebraic curve that is the hyperbolism of a circle with respect to its center and a tangent.

Kulp Quartic Curve implicit equation:
\[ x^{2} y^{2} + y^{2} a^{2} - a^{4} = 0 \]
Kulp Quartic Curve Cartesian equation:
\[ y = \pm \dfrac{a^{2}}{\sqrt{x^{2} + a^{2}}} \]

History and Applications

The Külp quartic1,2, also known as Kulp's quartic or the Külp conchoid, was studied by the German physicist Ludwig Külp (1835–1891) in 1868 in Archiv der Math. und Physik. It was later examined by Goormaghtigh in 1913. The curve is the hyperbolism of a circle with respect to its center and a tangent and is a special case of Granville's egg. It bears a resemblance to the conchoid of Nicomedes.

No real-life applications of the Kulp quartic in engineering or the natural sciences were identified in the primary mathematical references.

AutoLISP Code

(defun C:KULP (/ basept xaxisangle cd sa sb sm om os divcount cosangle sinangle x0 y0 plineents segcount baseinfo paramstep
                 divpt xspan restartflag id iq dcl opt kw mode dpr dct dpt span eq_display xc yc a1 a2 x1 y1 x2 y2 fa)
  (setq id "Kulp Quartic"
        iq "y=±a²/√(x²+a²)"
        dcl nil
        opt nil
        kw nil
        mode 'param
        dpr "# of divisions to division point"
        dct 200
        dpt '(2.0 0.0)
        span nil
        kw (ca id iq dcl opt kw mode dpr dct dpt span)
        fa (get_real 2 "a (scale)" 1.0)
        eq_display (strcat "\na=" (rtos fa 2 4))
        xc '(eval a2)
        yc '(/ (* fa fa) (alt_expt (+ (* (eval a2) (eval a2)) (* fa fa)) 0.5 T))
        paramstep (/ xspan divcount)
        a1 0
        a2 a1
        x1 0
        x2 x1
        y1 (eval yc)
        a2 '(+ a1 paramstep)
        x2 '(eval xc)
        y2 '(eval yc))
  (cc))
(C:KULP)
