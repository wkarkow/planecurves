Swastika Curve

A decorative polar curve \(r^{2}=\tan((p/(2q))\theta)\) whose reduced numerator \(p\) sets the number of hooked arms and whose reduced denominator \(q\) controls how those arms overlap as further revolutions are traced.

Swastika Curve polar equation:
\[ r^{2} = \tan\left(\frac{p}{2q}\,\theta\right) \]

History and Applications

The swastika curve is a decorative polar curve intended to evoke the rotational, hooked-arm form long known as the swastika. The word derives from Sanskrit (svastika, “conducive to well-being”)1, and the symbol appears across much of Eurasia from prehistoric times: in ancient Indian religious art, on Greco-Roman and Celtic artifacts, in East Asian motifs, and in numerous folk traditions.2,3 Its geometry expresses rotation and continuity, often interpreted as solar motion, cyclic time, or auspiciousness.2,3 Variants include three-armed rotational forms (related to the classical triskelion)4 as well as four-armed right-angled forms familiar from later history. The modern Nazi appropriation of the symbol in the 20th century represents only a recent specific use of a far older symbol.1 In the classical parameter setting (integer \(p/q\)), this swastika curve produces a symmetric hooked form suggestive of the traditional four-armed design. For small fractional parameters, the curve can resemble tightly wound barred spiral galaxies, with a central straight bar and outward-flaring spiral arms.

When \(p/q\) is written in lowest terms, the reduced numerator \(p\) gives the number of distinct arms; the reduced denominator \(q\) controls how many arms overlap as additional revolutions are traced. A single straight “bar” through the origin, in the sense of a barred spiral galaxy, occurs only when \(p/q = 1\) (naturally) or \(p/q = \tfrac{1}{2}\) with mirroring and \(q > p\); larger values of \(p\) produce multiple radial directions rather than a single barred axis.

AutoLISP Code

(defun C:SWASTIKA (/ basept xaxisangle cd sa sb sm om os divcount cosangle sinangle x0 y0 plineents segcount baseinfo paramstep divpt
                     xstep restartflag id iq dcl opt kw mode dpr dct dpt span eq_display xc yc skm a1 a2 x1 y1 x2 y2 maxdraw rval fa fb)
  (setq id "Swastika Curve"
        iq "r²=tan((p/(2q))θ)"
        dcl nil
        opt nil
        kw nil
        mode 'polar
        dpr "# of divisions around circle"
        dct 360
        dpt nil
        span 360
        kw (ca id iq dcl opt kw mode dpr dct dpt span)
        fa (get_real 2 "p (numerator, # of arms when reduced)" 4.0)
        fb (get_real 2 "q (denominator, increases spiralling)" 1.0)
        eq_display (strcat "\np=" (rtos fa 2 4) ", q=" (rtos fb 2 4) ")")
        maxdraw (get_real 2 "Maximum x and y values allowed?" 3.5)
        rval '(if (< (abs (tan (* (/ fa (* 2.0 fb)) (eval a2)))) 1e-9) 0.0 (if (< (tan (* (/ fa (* 2.0 fb)) (eval a2))) 0.0) 0.0 (sqrt (tan (* (/ fa (* 2.0 fb)) (eval a2))))))
        xc '(* (eval rval) (cos (eval a2)))
        yc '(* (eval rval) (sin (eval a2)))
        skm '(T T nil)
        a1 0
        a2 a1
        x1 (eval xc)
        y1 (eval yc)
        a2 '(+ a1 paramstep)
        x2 '(eval xc)
        y2 '(eval yc))
  (cc))
(C:SWASTIKA)
