Holditch Parabola

The locus of the midpoint of a chord of fixed length 2c that slides with its endpoints along a standard parabola of focal length f.

Holditch Parabola equation:
\[ x = \dfrac{y^{2}}{4f} + \dfrac{f\,c^{2}}{y^{2}+4f^{2}} \]

History and Applications

The Holditch parabola is the locus of the midpoint of a chord of fixed length 2c that slides with its endpoints along a standard parabola of focal length \(f\) (vertex to focus). It is a special case of the Holditch curves that arise from the construction in Holditch’s theorem. Rev. Hamnet Holditch (1800–1867), an English mathematician and president of Gonville and Caius College, Cambridge, published the theorem in 1858.1,2 The theorem states that if a chord of constant length slides inside a closed convex curve, the area between the original curve and the locus of a point dividing the chord into segments of lengths \(p\) and \(q\) equals \(\pi \cdot p \cdot q\), independent of the shape or size of the base curve. For the midpoint case treated here, \(p=q=c\).

Historically these constructions belong to the broader nineteenth-century study of glissettes—loci generated by constrained sliding motions. The parabolic case shows how the sliding constraint interacts with curvature: the parameter \(f\) sets the focal scale of the generating parabola, and the ratio \( \frac{c}{f}\) governs whether the induced quartic remains convex or develops inflection and dimpling.

Real-life applications of Holditch curves and the underlying theorem appear in computational geometry and computer-aided design. The theorem has been used to compute areas between Bézier curves and quadratic uniform B-spline curves in CAGD/CAM systems.3 Generalized versions appear in kinematic analysis of mechanisms that contain a rigid link whose endpoints move along a prescribed guide.

AutoLISP Code

(defun C:HOLDITCHPARABOLA (/ basept xaxisangle cd sa sb sm om os divcount cosangle sinangle x0 y0 plineents segcount baseinfo paramstep
                             restartflag id iq dcl opt kw mode dpr dct dpt span eq_display xc yc skm a1 a2 x1 y1 x2 y2 fa fb fc)
  (setq id "Holditch Parabola"
        iq "x=y²/(4·f)+(f·c²)/(y²+4·f²)"
        dcl nil
        opt nil
        kw nil
        mode 'param
        dpr "# of divisions for y from 0 to 1"
        dct 100
        dpt nil
        span nil
        kw (ca id iq dcl opt kw mode dpr dct dpt span)
        fa (get_real 2 "f (focal length, vertex to focus)" 1.0)
        fb (get_real 0 "c (half-chord length)" 1.0)
        eq_display (strcat "\nf=" (rtos fa 2 4) ", c=" (rtos fb 2 4))
        xc '(progn (setq fc (eval a2)) (+ (/ (* fc fc) (* 4.0 fa)) (/ (* 2 fa fb fb) (* 2.0 (+ (* fc fc) (* 4 fa fa))))))
        yc '(eval a2)
        paramstep (/ 1.0 divcount)
        skm '(T nil T)
        a1 0
        a2 a1
        x1 (eval xc)
        y1 (eval yc)
        a2 '(+ a1 paramstep)
        x2 '(eval xc)
        y2 '(eval yc))
  (cc))
(C:HOLDITCHPARABOLA)
