Trifolium

A three-leaved algebraic curve of degree four, generated as the pedal of a deltoid with respect to a point inside the deltoid.

Trifolium implicit equation:
\[ (x^{2}+y^{2})^{2} = (x^{2}+y^{2})(ax+by) + rx(x^{2}-3y^{2}) \]
Trifolium polar equation:
\[ \rho = r \cdot \cos(3\theta) + a \cdot \cos(\theta) + b \cdot \sin(\theta) \]

History and Applications

The Trifolium1,2, a three-leaved algebraic curve of degree four, was studied by G. de Longchamps in 18873 and by Brocard in 1891. It belongs to the broader family of folia and is generated geometrically as the pedal of a deltoid, a three-cusped hypocycloid, taken with respect to an arbitrary point inside the deltoid. When the chosen pedal point lies at the deltoid’s center, the result is the regular centered trifolium whose three leaves meet at the origin. For any other interior pedal point the triple point (the location where the three leaves intersect and three distinct tangents meet) is displaced from the origin, producing a translated but otherwise identical three-leaved shape.

The polar and Cartesian equations coded for this entry are the general forms that describe the entire family. The parameters \( a \) and \( b \) directly set the coordinates of the triple point relative to the chosen origin, so every rigid translation of the classic three-leaved curve is included. The special case \( a = b = 0 \) recovers precisely the regular trifolium already treated in the related Folium entry. This generality distinguishes the present implementation from treatments limited to the centered rose form.

Documented applications in engineering and the natural sciences remain limited; the curve has functioned chiefly as a classical object in algebraic geometry and the study of pedals and envelopes. Modern mathematical work continues to examine algebraic properties of its offsets, but no widespread practical uses in mechanisms, optics, or other technical fields have been established.

(defun C:TRIFOLIUM (/ basept xaxisangle cd sa sb sm om os divcount cosangle sinangle x0 y0 plineents segcount baseinfo paramstep
                     restartflag rval id iq dcl opt kw mode dpr dct dpt span xc yc skm a1 a2 x1 y1 x2 y2 eq_display fa fb fc)
  (setq id "Trifolium"
        iq "ρ=r·cos(3θ)+a·cos(θ)+b·sin(θ)"
        dcl nil
        opt nil
        kw nil
        mode 'polar
        dpr "# of divisions around circle"
        dct 720
        dpt nil
        span 360
        kw (ca id iq dcl opt kw mode dpr dct dpt span)
        fa (get_real 2 "r (triple-term scale)" 1.0)
        fb (get_real 2 "a (x-offset coefficient)" 0.0)
        fc (get_real 2 "b (y-offset coefficient)" 0.0)
        eq_display (strcat "\nr=" (rtos fa 2 4) ", a=" (rtos fb 2 4) ", b=" (rtos fc 2 4)))
  (setq rval '(+ (* fa (cos (* 3.0 (eval a2)))) (* fb (cos (eval a2))) (* fc (sin (eval a2))))
        xc '(* (eval rval) (cos (eval a2)))
        yc '(* (eval rval) (sin (eval a2)))
        skm '(T T T)
        a1 0
        a2 a1
        x1 (eval xc)
        y1 (eval yc)
        a2 '(+ a1 paramstep)
        x2 '(eval xc)
        y2 '(eval yc))
  (cc))
