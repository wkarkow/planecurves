Bow Curve

A quartic plane algebraic curve resembling a decorative bow or ribbon with a triple point at the origin.

Bow Curve implicit equation:
\[ x^{4} = a (x^{2} y - y^{3}) \]
Bow Curve parametric equations:
\[ x = a (t - t^{3}), \quad y = a (t^{2} - t^{4}) \]

History and Applications

The bow curve1 is a quartic plane curve named for its resemblance to a decorative bow or ribbon. It has a triple point at the origin where three branches meet and is therefore a rational curve of genus zero2. It does not appear to be associated with any particular historical mathematician. Instead, it is one of many plane algebraic curves collected in the 20th century for their interesting shapes and properties. It is listed in Cundy and Rollett’s Mathematical Models3 among other quartic curves suitable for physical models or drafting study.

The curve is included in catalogues of quartic and ornamental curves4. Its parametric representation makes it straightforward to generate with computer drafting tools such as AutoCAD using basic AutoLISP. The implicit Cartesian equation confirms its degree-four algebraic character. Detailed properties, including locations of horizontal and vertical tangents and the area enclosed by its loops, are available for the unit case (a = 1)1.

No real-life applications in engineering or the natural sciences were found in the primary mathematical references or broader searches.

AutoLISP Code

(defun C:BOWCURVE (/ basept xaxisangle cd sa sb sm om os divcount cosangle sinangle x0 y0 plineents segcount baseinfo paramstep
                     divpt restartflag id iq dcl opt kw mode dpr dct dpt span eq_display xc yc skm a1 a2 x1 y1 x2 y2 fa fb)
  (setq id "Bow Curve"
        iq "x=a·(t-t³), y=a·(t²-t⁴)"
        dcl nil
        opt nil
        kw nil
        mode 'param
        dpr "# of divisions for t from 0 to tmax"
        dct 500
        dpt nil
        span nil
        kw (ca id iq dcl opt kw mode dpr dct dpt span)
        fa (get_real 2 "a (scale)" 2.0)
        fb (get_real 2 "t max (>1 adds downward tails)" 1.125)
        eq_display (strcat "\na=" (rtos fa 2 4) ", t max=" (rtos fb 2 4))
        xc '(* fa (- (eval a2) (* (eval a2) (eval a2) (eval a2))))
        yc '(* fa (- (* (eval a2) (eval a2)) (* (eval a2) (eval a2) (eval a2) (eval a2))))
        span fb
        paramstep (/ span divcount)
        skm '(nil T T)
        a1 0
        a2 a1
        x1 (eval xc)
        y1 (eval yc)
        a2 '(+ a1 paramstep)
        x2 '(eval xc)
        y2 '(eval yc))
  (cc))
(C:BOWCURVE)
