Constant Angular Acceleration Curve

Trajectory of a point moving with uniformly accelerated motion along a straight line that rotates at constant angular velocity about a fixed point.

Constant Angular Acceleration Curve differential equation:
\[ \dfrac{d^{2}s}{d\theta^{2}} = a \]
Equivalent first-order form (polar):
\[ \left(\dfrac{dr}{d\theta}\right)^{2} + r^{2} = a^{2}\theta^{2} \]

History and Applications

The Constant Angular Acceleration Curve1 was studied by Mikhail Gaichenkov in 2008 and is defined by the geometric condition \(d^{2}s/d\theta^{2} = a\), meaning that arc length increases with constant angular acceleration. It describes the trajectory of a point moving with uniformly accelerated motion along a straight line while that line rotates with constant angular velocity around a fixed point. Unlike classical spirals of the 17th and 18th centuries, this curve arises from a modern differential constraint rather than an explicit polar formula. Its solution satisfies \(r'^{2} + r^{2} = a^{2}\theta^{2}\) and, while not expressible in elementary closed form, possesses a convergent Maclaurin series near the origin. As \(a\) approaches zero the curve approaches the Galileo spiral. As \(\theta\) increases, the curve approaches the spacing of an Archimedean spiral.

No specific real-life applications in engineering or the natural sciences were identified for this exact curve in primary mathematical references. It remains a theoretical model for kinematic trajectories that combine linear acceleration with rotation.

AutoLISP Code

(defun C:ANGULARACCEL (/ basept xaxisangle cd sa sb sm om os divcount cosangle sinangle x0 y0 plineents segcount baseinfo paramstep restartflag
                         id iq dcl opt kw mode dpr dct dpt span eq_display xc yc skm a1 a2 x1 y1 x2 y2 fa fb fc fd fe ff fg fh fi fj)
  (setq id "Constant Angular Acceleration Curve"
        iq "d²s/dθ²=a"
        dcl nil
        opt nil
        kw nil
        mode 'polar
        dpr "# of divisions around circle"
        dct 360
        dpt nil
        span 360
        kw (ca id iq dcl opt kw mode dpr dct dpt span)
        fa (get_real 2 "a (angular acceleration)" 1.0)
        eq_display (strcat "\na=" (rtos fa 2 4))
        fb 0.0
        fc 0.0
        xc '(* fb (cos fc))
        yc '(* fb (sin fc))
        skm '(T T nil)
        a1 0.0
        a2 a1
        x1 (eval xc)
        y1 (eval yc)
        a2 '(progn
              (setq fd paramstep
                    fe fc
                    ff fb
                    fg (- (* fa fa fe fe) (* ff ff))
                    fh (+ fe (* 0.5 fd))
                    fi (+ ff (* 0.5 fd (if (< fg 1e-12) 0.0 (sqrt fg))))
                    fj (- (* fa fa fh fh) (* fi fi))
                    fb (+ ff (* fd (if (< fj 1e-12) 0.0 (sqrt fj))))
                    fc (+ fc fd))
              fc)
        x2 '(eval xc)
        y2 '(eval yc))
  (cc))
