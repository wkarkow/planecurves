Lazennec Curve

A cardioid variant of Jerabek’s bicorn construction, resembling an upswept bicorn.

Lazennec Curve parametric equations:
\[ x=4a\frac{\cos t\ (2-\sin t\cos^{2}t)}{2+\sin t+2\cos^{2}t},\quad y=-4a\frac{1+\cos^{2}t+\cos^{4}t}{2+\sin t+2\cos^{2}t} \]

History and Applications

The Lazennec curve1 appears only as a late note on the MathCurve bicorn page. It is obtained by applying the 1912 construction of V. Jerabek (a variable diameter of a circle whose ends are joined to two fixed points, the intersection of those lines tracing a bicorn for suitable parameters) after the circle has been replaced by a cardioid. The concrete construction uses the four points \(A(-4,0)\), \(B(4,0)\), \(C(0,-3)\) and \(D(0,-2)\). A segment \([PQ]\) of fixed length 4 is constrained to pass through \(D\) while its midpoint \(I\) travels on the circle of centre \(C\) and radius 1. The endpoints \(P\) and \(Q\) then describe a cardioid with cusp at \(D\). As the segment turns, the intersection \(M\) of the lines \((AP)\) and \((BQ)\) traces a closed curve that resembles the classical bicorn but with two upswept cusps.

Whereas the bicorn resembles the cocked hat of an 1812 British army officer, the Lazennec curve more closely resembles the École Polytechnique student hat.2 No further historical attribution, independent catalogue entry, or Cartesian equation is given. Searches of the principal plane-curve references and the wider literature locate no other mention of Henri Lazennec in connection with this locus, nor any application in engineering or the natural sciences.

AutoLISP Code

(defun C:LAZENNEC (/ basept xaxisangle cd sa sb sm om os divcount cosangle sinangle x0 y0 plineents segcount baseinfo paramstep
                     divpt restartflag id iq dcl opt kw mode dpr dct dpt span eq_display xc yc skm a1 a2 x1 y1 x2 y2 fa)
  (setq id "Lazennec Curve"
        iq "x=4·a·cos(t)(2-sin(t)cos²(t))/(2+sin(t)+2cos²(t)), y=-4·a·(1+cos²(t)+cos⁴(t))/(2+sin(t)+2cos²(t))"
        dcl nil
        opt nil
        kw nil
        mode 'polar
        dpr "# of divisions around circle"
        dct 360
        dpt nil
        span 360
        kw (ca id iq dcl opt kw mode dpr dct dpt span)
        fa (get_real 2 "a (overall scale)" 1.0)
        eq_display (strcat "\na=" (rtos fa 2 4))
        xc '(* fa 4.0 (/ (* (cos (eval a2)) (- 2.0 (* (sin (eval a2)) (expt (cos (eval a2)) 2)))) (+ 2.0 (sin (eval a2)) (* 2.0 (expt (cos (eval a2)) 2)))))
        yc '(* fa -4.0 (/ (+ 1.0 (expt (cos (eval a2)) 2) (expt (cos (eval a2)) 4)) (+ 2.0 (sin (eval a2)) (* 2.0 (expt (cos (eval a2)) 2)))))
        skm '(T T T)
        a1 0.0
        a2 a1
        x1 (eval xc)
        y1 (eval yc)
        a2 '(+ a1 paramstep)
        x2 '(eval xc)
        y2 '(eval yc))
  (cc))
(C:LAZENNEC)
