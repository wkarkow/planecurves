Bifoliate

A quartic plane algebraic curve with a cusp at the origin.

Bifoliate implicit equation:
\[ x^{4} + y^{4} = 2 a x y^{2} \]
Bifoliate polar equation:
\[ r(\theta) = a \cdot \frac{8 \cos(\theta) \sin^{2}(\theta)}{3 + \cos(4\theta)} \]

History and Applications

The Bifoliate curve1, also known as the double folium, is a quartic plane curve studied by French mathematicians Gabriel-Marie de Longchamps in 1886 and Henri Brocard in 1887.2 It is the pedal curve of the deltoid with respect to one of its points.1 The curve exhibits a cusp at the origin and is symmetric about the x-axis in its standard position.2,3

No real-life applications in engineering or the natural sciences were identified in primary mathematical references.
