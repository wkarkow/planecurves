Tractory of an Ellipse

The path of the rear end of a fixed-length leash whose front end follows an ellipse.

Tractory of an Ellipse differential equations:
\[ \begin{cases} (x-x_0)\,dy=(y-y_0)\,dx \\ (x-x_0)^2+(y-y_0)^2=L^2 \\ \dfrac{x_0^2}{a^2}+\dfrac{y_0^2}{b^2}=1 \end{cases} \]
Cartesian parametrization (numerical):
\[ \begin{cases} x=x_0-L\cos(\varphi_0-\alpha) \\ y=y_0-L\sin(\varphi_0-\alpha) \end{cases} \]
where \(\varphi_0\) is the base tangential angle and
\[ \dfrac{d\alpha}{ds_0}=\dfrac{1}{R_{c0}}-\dfrac{\sin\alpha}{L} \]

History and Applications

The tractory (or tractoire) is the curve traced by a point \(M\) that is pulled at constant distance \(L\) (the leash) by a point \(M_0\) moving along a given base curve, with the leash remaining tangent to the path of \(M\). Equivalently, the base is the equitangential curve of the tractory: a fixed length is marked off along each tangent to the tractory to recover the base.1 The classical case is the tractory of a straight line, which is the tractrix studied by Huygens in 1692 and later by Leibniz and Johann Bernoulli. The next specimen treated in the literature is the tractory of a circle, examined by Bordoni in 1820; when the leash equals the radius one obtains the tractrix spiral, and when the leash is longer one obtains a multi-cusped curve that closes precisely when the ratio of leash to radius is rational.2 No historical source examined attributes a special study, name, or closed-form treatment to the tractory of an ellipse. The general differential condition applies equally to an elliptic base, but the variable curvature of the ellipse prevents the elementary integrations that exist for the line and the circle. The curves drawn by the accompanying code are therefore numerical computation solutions arising from that general definition. One who experiments with this code will find that a leash length producing closure for a particular period number is not always found by the computation for that period but is located by changing the period entered and then re-entering a period appropriate for one full cycle. By manually varying the leash length once approximate closure is achieved, one can fine-tune the closure to any desired degree of precision.

The same mechanical interpretation that attaches to every tractory remains valid: the rear wheels of a vehicle (or the rear wheel of a bicycle) follow a tractory of the path taken by the front wheels. In the steady-state equal-speed pursuit of a dog after a hare the pursuit curve is likewise a tractory of the hare’s path. No engineering or scientific application peculiar to an elliptic base was found.

  (defun STEPPER_END (a b c / R P0 P1 phi0 theta Ux Uy P_prev P_new dP U dot dR vec len maxr rdist)
    (if (and a (> (length a) 1) (> b 1e-6))
      (progn
        (setq P0 (car a)
              P1 (cadr a)
              phi0 (atan (- (cadr P1) (cadr P0)) (- (car P1) (car P0)))
              theta (- phi0 c)
              Ux (cos theta)
              Uy (sin theta)
              R (list (- (car P0) (* b Ux)) (- (cadr P0) (* b Uy)))
              P_prev P0
              maxr (distance R '(0.0 0.0)))
        (foreach P_new (cdr a)
          (setq dP (list (- (car P_new) (car P_prev)) (- (cadr P_new) (cadr P_prev)))
                U (list (/ (- (car P_prev) (car R)) b) (/ (- (cadr P_prev) (cadr R)) b))
                dot (+ (* (car dP) (car U)) (* (cadr dP) (cadr U)))
                dR (list (* dot (car U)) (* dot (cadr U)))
                R (list (+ (car R) (car dR)) (+ (cadr R) (cadr dR)))
                vec (list (- (car P_new) (car R)) (- (cadr P_new) (cadr R)))
                len (sqrt (+ (* (car vec) (car vec)) (* (cadr vec) (cadr vec)))))
          (if (> len 1e-9) (setq R (list (- (car P_new) (* b (/ (car vec) len))) (- (cadr P_new) (* b (/ (cadr vec) len))))))
          (setq rdist (distance R '(0.0 0.0)))
          (if (> rdist maxr) (setq maxr rdist))
          (setq P_prev P_new))
        (cons R maxr))
      nil))
  (defun DRAW_TRACTORY (a b c / R P0 P1 phi0 theta Ux Uy pts-tract P_prev P_new dP U dot dR vec len pts-out)
    (if (and a (> (length a) 1) (> b 1e-6))
      (progn
        (setq P0 (car a)
              P1 (cadr a)
              phi0 (atan (- (cadr P1) (cadr P0)) (- (car P1) (car P0)))
              theta (- phi0 c)
              Ux (cos theta)
              Uy (sin theta)
              R (list (- (car P0) (* b Ux))
                      (- (cadr P0) (* b Uy)))
              P_prev P0)
        (setq pts-tract (list R))
        (foreach P_new (cdr a)
          (setq dP (list (- (car P_new) (car P_prev)) (- (cadr P_new) (cadr P_prev)))
                U (list (/ (- (car P_prev) (car R)) b) (/ (- (cadr P_prev) (cadr R)) b))
                dot (+ (* (car dP) (car U)) (* (cadr dP) (cadr U)))
                dR (list (* dot (car U)) (* dot (cadr U)))
                R (list (+ (car R) (car dR)) (+ (cadr R) (cadr dR)))
                vec (list (- (car P_new) (car R)) (- (cadr P_new) (cadr R)))
                len (sqrt (+ (* (car vec) (car vec)) (* (cadr vec) (cadr vec)))))
          (if (> len 1e-9) (setq R (list (- (car P_new) (* b (/ (car vec) len))) (- (cadr P_new) (* b (/ (cadr vec) len))))))
          (setq pts-tract (cons R pts-tract)
                P_prev P_new))
        (setq pts-tract (reverse pts-tract))
        (setq pts-out (mapcar '(lambda (p) (cons 10 p)) pts-tract))
        (entmake
          (append
            (list '(0 . "LWPOLYLINE")
                  '(100 . "AcDbEntity")
                  '(62 . 1)
                  '(100 . "AcDbPolyline")

                  '(70 . 0))

            '((210 0.0 0.0 1.0))))
        (princ (strcat "\nTractory: " (itoa (length pts-tract)) " pts")))
      (princ "\nInsufficient base points or leash.")))
  (setq id "Tractory of an Ellipse"
        iq "numerical tractory of ellipse base"
        dcl nil
        opt nil
        kw 0
        mode 'param
        dpr "# of divisions in one base period"
        dct 2160
        dpt nil
        span nil
        kw (ca id iq dcl opt kw mode dpr dct dpt span)
        fa (get_real 6 "a (semi-major)" 3.0)
        fb (get_real 6 "b (semi-minor)" 2.0)
        fc (get_integer 0 "n (n periods = n-1 petals if closed)" 3)
        eq_display (strcat "\na=" (rtos fa 2 4) ", b=" (rtos fb 2 4) ", n=" (itoa fc))
        paramstep (/ (* 2.0 pi) 360)
        fd '()
        tt 0.0)
  (repeat (1+ 360)
    (setq x0 (* fa (cos tt))
          y0 (* fb (sin tt))
          fd (cons (list x0 y0) fd)
          tt (+ tt paramstep)))
  (setq fd (reverse fd))
  (if (and (> (length fd) 1) (equal (car fd) (last fd) 1e-6)) (setq fd (reverse (cdr (reverse fd)))))
  (setq fe fd)
  (if (> fc 1) (repeat (1- fc) (setq fe (append fe fd))))
  (setq Lmin (min fa fb)
        Lmax (* 4.0 (max fa fb))
        fg (/ (- Lmax Lmin) 40.0)
        ff '()

        fj (+ Lmin 0.0))
  (princ "\nCoarse search for candidate leashes...")
  (while (<= fi Lmax)

              Uy (sin theta)

        (if (and (>= fi fj) (< maxr (* 5.0 fa)) (< err 10.0)) (setq ff (cons (list fi err maxr) ff)))))
    (setq fi (+ fi fg)))
  (defun sort-by-err (lst / sorted item)
    (setq sorted '())
    (foreach item lst (setq sorted (insert-sorted item sorted))) sorted)
  (defun insert-sorted (item lst / head tail)
    (cond
      ((null lst) (list item))
      ((< (cadr item) (cadr (car lst))) (cons item lst))
      (t (cons (car lst) (insert-sorted item (cdr lst))))))
  (setq ff (sort-by-err ff))
  (if (> (length ff) 8)
    (progn
      (setq tmp '() i 0)
      (foreach cand ff
        (if (< i 8) (setq tmp (cons cand tmp) i (1+ i))))
      (setq ff (reverse tmp))))
  (if ff
    (progn
      (princ "\nCandidate leashes (L, closure error, max radius):")
      (setq idx 1)
      (foreach cand ff
        (princ (strcat "\n  " (itoa idx) ": L=" (rtos (car cand) 2 4) "  err=" (rtos (cadr cand) 2 4) "  maxr=" (rtos (caddr cand) 2 2)))

      (setq pick (get_integer 0 "Select candidate number (0 to enter L manually)" 1))

  (setq phi (/ (- (sqrt 5.0) 1.0) 2.0)
        a (max fj (- fn (* 1.5 fg)))

        tol 1e-6
        iter 0
        fp 1e99)

    (if ft
      (progn

              Ux (cos (- phi0 0.0)) Uy (sin (- phi0 0.0))

      (setq b d d c c (+ a (* (- b a) (- 1.0 phi))) fn c fp fw)
      (setq a c c d d (+ a (* (- b a) phi)) fn d fp fx))

  (princ (strcat "\nBest L ≈ " (rtos fn 2 6) " (closure error " (rtos fp 2 8) ")"))

        fz (get_real 6 "alpha (°)" 0.0)

  (repeat (1+ divcount)
    (setq x0 (* fa (cos tt))
          y0 (* fb (sin tt))
          fm (cons (list x0 y0) fm)

  (if (and (> (length fm) 1) (equal (car fm) (last fm) 1e-6)) (setq fm (reverse (cdr (reverse fm)))))
  (setq fl fm)
  (if (> fc 1)
    (repeat (1- fc)
      (setq fl (append fl fm))))
  (setq fl (append fl (list (car fm)))
        fk (mapcar '(lambda (p) (cons 10 p)) fm))
  (entmake
    (append
      (list '(0 . "LWPOLYLINE")
            '(100 . "AcDbEntity")
            '(62 . 7)
            '(100 . "AcDbPolyline")
            (cons 90 (length fk))
            '(70 . 1))
      fk
      '((210 0.0 0.0 1.0))))
  (princ (strcat "\nBase ellipse: " (itoa (length fm)) " pts, a=" (rtos fa 2 4) ", b=" (rtos fb 2 4) ", n=" (rtos fc 2 4) ", leash=" (rtos leash 2 8) ", α=" (rtos fz 2 4) "°"))
