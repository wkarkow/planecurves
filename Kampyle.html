Kampyle of Eudoxus

A classical quartic plane curve studied by Eudoxus in connection with the duplication of the cube.

Kampyle of Eudoxus implicit equation:
\[ x^{4} = a^{2}(x^{2} + y^{2}) \]
Kampyle of Eudoxus polar equation:
\[ r = a \cdot \sec^{2}(\theta) \]
Generalized polar equation:
\[ r = a \cdot \sec^{b}(c\theta) \]

History and Applications

The Kampyle of Eudoxus1,2,3, named after Eudoxus of Cnidus (c. 408 BC – c. 355 BC), a Greek astronomer, mathematician, and philosopher who was a pupil of Plato, is a quartic plane curve. Eudoxus studied the curve in connection with the classical problem of the duplication of the cube, one of the three famous problems of ancient Greek mathematics. Eudoxus is also known for his contributions to the method of exhaustion, an early precursor to integration concepts in calculus that determines areas and volumes through successive approximations, and for formulas measuring volumes of pyramids, cones, and cylinders. The curve is sometimes called Clairaut’s curve. It is symmetric about both the x-axis and y-axis and has parabolic asymptotes. Geometrically, it is the radial curve of the catenary (up to a rotation by \(\frac{\pi}{2}\)) and the locus of the focus of a parabola constrained to remain tangent to a fixed straight line at a fixed point (a glissette). It also arises as the rolling curve of linear conchoidal motion and as the base curve of Kappa motion.

Real-life applications in engineering and the natural sciences are not documented in primary mathematical references. Its significance remains primarily historical and theoretical within classical geometry and the study of special plane curves.

AutoLISP Code

(defun C:KAMPYLE (/ basept xaxisangle cd sa sb sm om os divcount cosangle sinangle x0 y0 plineents segcount baseinfo divpt xstep paramstep
                    restartflag id iq dcl opt kw mode dpr dct dpt span eq_display rval xc yc skm a1 a2 x1 y1 x2 y2 maxdraw fa fb fc)
  (setq id "Kampyle of Eudoxus"
        iq "r=a·secᵇ(cθ)"
        dcl nil
        opt nil
        kw nil
        mode 'polar
        dpr "# of divisions around circle"
        dct 360
        dpt nil
        span 360
        kw (ca id iq dcl opt kw mode dpr dct dpt span)
        fa (get_real 2 "a (scale)" 1.0)
        fb (get_real 2 "b (power of sec)" 2.0)
        fc (get_real 2 "c (coefficient of θ)" 1.0)
        eq_display (strcat "\na= " (rtos fa 2 4))
        rval '(* fa (alt_expt (/ 1.0 (avoid_zero (cos (* fc (eval a2))))) fb nil))
        xc '(* (eval rval) (cos (eval a2)))
        yc '(* (eval rval) (sin (eval a2)))
        skm '(nil T T)
        maxdraw (get_real 2 "Maximum x and y values allowed?" 50)
        a1 0
        a2 a1
        x1 (eval xc)
        y1 (eval yc)
        a2 '(+ a1 paramstep)
        x2 '(eval xc)
        y2 '(eval yc))
  (cc))
(C:KAMPYLE)
