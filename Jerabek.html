Jerabek Curve

The locus of the free endpoint of a right angle whose vertex traces a circle while remaining collinear with a fixed origin.

Jerabek Curve polar equation:
\[ r = a \dfrac{k \cos\theta - 1}{k - \cos\theta} \]
Generalized polar equation:
\[ r = a \dfrac{k \cos^{p}(m\theta) - c}{k - \cos^{p}(m\theta)} \]

History and Applications

The Jerabek curve is a plane curve defined geometrically as the locus of the endpoint M of a right angle PAM, where point P traces a circle of radius a centered at O, M lies on the line OP, and A is fixed at (a, 0). It was studied by Czech mathematician Václav Jeřábek (1845–1931), who specialized in constructive geometry, together with Neuberg in 1885. The curve is a circular rational quartic and serves as the base of the circular conchoidal movement of a plane over a fixed plane. It is also the conchoid of a centered conic taken with pole at a focus and modulus equal to the semi-major axis.1,2

In mechanical engineering and kinematics, the Jerabek curve serves as the rolling curve (or base curve) for the slider-crank mechanism, a linkage used in reciprocating engines, pumps, and compressors to convert rotary motion to linear motion or vice versa.3

; JERABEK.LSP - started 11/20/25

(defun C:JERABEK (/ basept xaxisangle cd sa sb sm om os divcount cosangle sinangle x0 y0 plineents segcount baseinfo divpt xstep paramstep
                    restartflag id iq dcl opt kw mode dpr dct dpt span eq_display xc yc skm a1 a2 x1 y1 x2 y2 fa fb fc fd fe maxdraw rval)
  (setq id "Jerabek Curve"
        iq ""
        dcl "JERABEK"
        opt '("Canonical" "New Values")
        kw 0
        mode 'polar
        dpr "# of divisions around circle"
        dct 360
        dpt nil
        span 360
        kw (ca id iq dcl opt kw mode dpr dct dpt span))
  (cond
   ((eq kw 0)                                ; Canonical
    (princ "\nJerabek curve (canonical): r(θ) = a·(k·cosθ - 1)/(k - cosθ)")
    (setq fa (get_real 2 "a (scale)" 1.0)
          fb (get_real 2 "k (parameter)" 0.5) 
          fc 1.0
          fd 1.0
          fe 1.0
          eq_display (strcat "a=" (rtos fa 2 4) ", k=" (rtos fb 2 4))))
   ((eq kw 1)                                ; New Values
    (princ "\nJerabek curve (generalized): r(θ) = a·(k·cos^p(mθ) - c)/(k - cos^p(mθ))")
    (setq fa (get_real 2 "a (scale)" 1.0)
          fb (get_real 2 "k (parameter)" 2.0)
          fc (get_real 2 "c (parameter)" 2.0)
          fd (get_real 6 "p (exponent)" 1.0)
          fe (get_real 2 "m (multiplier for θ)" 1.0)
          eq_display (strcat "a= " (rtos fa 2 4) ", k=" (rtos fb 2 4) ", c=" (rtos fc 2 4) ", p=" (rtos fd 2 4) ", m=" (rtos fe 2 4)))))
  (setq rval '(if (< (abs (- fb (alt_expt (cos (* fe (eval a2))) fd nil))) 1e-12) (if (< (abs (- (* fb fb) fc)) 1e-12) (* fa (- fb)) 1e7)
              (* fa (/ (- (* fb (alt_expt (cos (* fe (eval a2))) fd nil)) fc) (- fb (alt_expt (cos (* fe (eval a2))) fd nil)))))
        xc '(* (eval rval) (cos (eval a2)))
        yc '(* (eval rval) (sin (eval a2)))
        maxdraw (get_real 2 "Maximum x and y values allowed?" 50)
        skm '(T T T)
        a1 0
        a2 a1
        x1 (eval xc)
        y1 (eval yc)
        a2 '(+ a1 paramstep)
        x2 '(eval xc)
        y2 '(eval yc))
  (cc))
