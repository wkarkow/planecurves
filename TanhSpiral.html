Tanh Spiral

A polar curve that starts at the pole and approaches a circle of radius \( |a| \) as \( \theta \) tends to \( \pm\infty \).

Tanh Spiral Cartesian equation:
\[ r = a \cdot \tanh(b\theta) \]

History and Applications

The Tanh Spiral, also known as the Spiral of the hyperbolic tangent,1 is a polar curve that originates at the pole and asymptotically approaches a circle of radius \( |a| \) as the polar angle \( \theta \) tends to \( \pm\infty \). It is documented in modern catalogs of special plane curves as an example of a spiral possessing an asymptotic circle and emerged in the era of computational visualization and curve databases rather than in 18th- or 19th-century analytic geometry.

The equation produces a smooth, monotonic radial growth that begins steeply near the origin and rapidly levels off toward the limiting radius \( |a| \). The single parameter \( b \) governs the rapidity of this saturation: larger \( |b| \) causes the curve to reach nearly its full radial extent within a smaller angular range, after which the path continues to wind tightly around the asymptotic circle. This non-oscillatory transition from radial to essentially circular motion is a direct consequence of the hyperbolic tangent function’s well-known decay to its horizontal asymptotes and distinguishes the curve from spirals that oscillate or cross their limiting forms.

Real-life applications in engineering and natural sciences remain limited in the published literature. One documented use occurs in geometrical acoustics, where the hyperbolic tangent spiral supplies exact analytical ray paths for selected problems of sound propagation in oceanic waveguides.2 Variants that combine a logarithmic term with the hyperbolic tangent spiral (log-tanh spirals) have been implemented in professional astronomical image-analysis packages for decomposing and modeling barred spiral structures in galaxy images, including strongly lensed systems.

AutoLISP Code

(defun C:TANHSPIRAL (/ basept xaxisangle cd sa sb sm om os divcount cosangle sinangle x0 y0 plineents segcount baseinfo paramstep
                       restartflag rval id iq dcl opt kw mode dpr dct dpt span eq_display xc yc skm a1 a2 x1 y1 x2 y2 fa fb)
  (setq id "Tanh Spiral"
        iq "r=a·tanh(b·θ)"
        dcl nil
        opt nil
        kw nil
        mode 'polar
        dpr "# of divisions per turn"
        dct 360
        dpt nil
        span 360
        kw (ca id iq dcl opt kw mode dpr dct dpt span)
        fa (get_real 2 "a (limiting circle radius)" 1.0)
        fb (get_real 2 "b (approach rate to limit)" 1.0)
        eq_display (strcat "\na=" (rtos fa 2 4) ", b=" (rtos fb 2 4))
        rval '(* fa (tanh (* fb (eval a2))))
        xc '(* (eval rval) (cos (eval a2)))
        yc '(* (eval rval) (sin (eval a2)))
        skm '(nil T nil)
        a1 0
        a2 a1
        x1 (eval xc)
        y1 (eval yc)
        a2 '(+ a1 paramstep)
        x2 '(eval xc)
        y2 '(eval yc))
  (cc))
(C:TANHSPIRAL)
