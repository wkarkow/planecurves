Balance Spring Curve

A transcendental polar spiral erroneously presented as approximating the form of a clock balance spring.

Balance Spring Curve polar equation:
\[ r = \dfrac{a}{1 + k \, e^{m \theta}} \]

History and Applications

The balance spring curve is presented on MathCurve as a transcendental spiral whose polar equation approximates the form of a balance spring in a clock. The entry states that the curve appeared in the magazine of the Palais de la Découverte without a reference. Wikipedia and the standard literature of horology identify the coils of a balance spring (hairspring) with an Archimedean spiral rather than with the present equation.1

A balance spring is a thin ribbon of metal, usually of rectangular cross-section, wound into a flat spiral. One end is fixed to a collet on the balance staff; the other is fixed to the balance cock. The restoring torque that the spring exerts on the balance wheel is intended to be proportional to angular displacement so that the period of oscillation remains independent of amplitude (isochronism). An Archimedean spiral of constant pitch supplies that nearly linear characteristic; terminal curves of Phillips or Grossmann type are sometimes added to keep the center of gravity on the axis during expansion and contraction.2

The polar equation given on MathCurve produces a spiral that approaches a point asymptote at the origin in one direction of \(\theta\) and a circular asymptote of radius \(a\) in the other. No engineering or scientific application of this particular polar form has been found. Practical balance springs continue to be laid out as Archimedean spirals.

References

(defun C:BALANCESPRING (/ basept xaxisangle cd sa sb sm om os divcount cosangle sinangle x0 y0 plineents segcount baseinfo paramstep

(defun r_s (a / b c)
  (if (not a)
    nil
    (progn
      (setq b (entnext a))
      (if b
        (progn
          (setq c (entget b))
          (if (= (cdr (assoc 0 c)) "VERTEX")
            (cdr (assoc 10 c))
            nil))
        nil))))
  (setq id "Balance Spring Curve"
        iq "r=a/(1+k*exp(m*theta))"
        dcl nil
        opt nil
        kw nil
        mode 'polar
        dpr "# of divisions around circle"
        dct 360
        dpt nil
        span 360
        kw (ca id iq dcl opt kw mode dpr dct dpt span)
        fa (get_real 2 "a (asymptotic radius)" 1.0)
        fb (get_real 2 "k (coefficient)" 1.0)
        fc (get_real 2 "m (exponent rate)" 0.1)
        fd (get_keyword 0 "Inward Outward" "Draw the Inward or Outward half?" "Inward")
        eq_display (strcat "\na=" (rtos fa 2 4) ", k=" (rtos fb 2 4) ", m=" (rtos fc 2 4)
                           ", " fd)
        rval '(/ fa (+ 1.0 (* fb (exp (* fc (eval a2))))))
        xc '(* (eval rval) (cos (eval a2)))
        yc '(* (eval rval) (sin (eval a2)))
        paramstep (if (eq fd "Inward") paramstep (- paramstep))
        skm '(T T T)
        a1 0.0
        a2 a1
        x1 (eval xc)
        y1 (eval yc)
        a2 '(+ a1 paramstep)
        x2 '(eval xc)
        y2 '(eval yc)
        fi (entlast))
  (cc)
  (setq fj (entlast))
  (if (and fi fj (not (eq fi fj)))
    (progn
      (setq fk (r_s fj))
      (setq prev_start (r_s fi))
      (if (and fk prev_start (equal fk prev_start 1e-6))
        (if (eq "Yes" (get_keyword 0 "Yes No" "Join to previous entity?" "No"))
          (command "pedit" fi "j" fj "" "")))))
  (princ))
