Reciprocal Power Exponential Curve

The Cartesian family \(y = a \cdot x^{b/x}\); for positive \(b\) the curve has a single maximum at \(x = e\) and then decays toward the axes.

Reciprocal Power Exponential Curve Cartesian equation:
\[ y = a \cdot x^{b/x} \]

History and Applications

The reciprocal power exponential curve, given in Cartesian form by \( y = a \cdot x^{b/x} \), arises as a generalization of the simpler case \( y = x^{1/x} \) (when \( a = 1 \) and \( b = 1 \)). This function appears in introductory calculus as an example for applying logarithmic differentiation or the first-derivative test to locate extrema. Taking the natural logarithm yields \( \ln(y) = (b/x)\ln(x) \), whose derivative reveals a critical point at \( x = e \) (for positive \( b \)), where the curve attains a maximum before decaying toward the axes. No specific mathematician is credited with its first study or naming; it functions as a pedagogical tool rather than a historically named curve or an entry in standard references. Rather, it is a variant within broader discussions of power or exponential families.1,2

No real-life applications in engineering and the natural sciences are documented in the reviewed literature. The curve serves primarily as a mathematical illustration rather than a model for observable phenomena.

AutoLISP Code

(defun C:RECIPPOWEREXP (/ basept xaxisangle cd sa sb sm om os divcount cosangle sinangle x0 y0 plineents segcount baseinfo paramstep
                          divpt restartflag xspan id iq dcl opt kw mode dpr dct dpt span eq_display xc yc a1 a2 x1 y1 x2 y2 fa fb)
  (setq id "Reciprocal Power Exponential Curve"
        iq "y=a·x^(b/x)"
        dcl nil
        opt nil
        kw nil
        mode 'param
        dpr "# of divisions to x-span of division point"
        dct 500
        dpt '(5.0 0.0)
        span nil
        kw (ca id iq dcl opt kw mode dpr dct dpt span)
        fa (get_real 2 "a (y-scale)" 1.0)
        fb (get_real 2 "b (exponent in b/x)" 1.0)
        fc (if (eq (get_keyword 0 "Positive Negative" "Positive or Negative x?" "Positive") "Positive") T nil)
        eq_display (strcat "\na=" (rtos fa 2 4) ", b=" (rtos fb 2 4))
        xc '(eval a2)
        yc '(* fa (alt_expt (eval a2) (/ fb (avoid_zero (eval a2))) nil))
        maxdraw (get_real 2 "Maximum x and y values allowed?" 1e5)
        span xspan
        paramstep (/ xspan divcount)
        paramstep (if fc paramstep (- paramstep))
        a1 0
        a2 a1
        x1 (eval xc)
        y1 (eval yc)
        a2 '(+ a1 paramstep)
        x2 '(eval xc)
        y2 '(eval yc))
  (cc))
(C:RECIPPOWEREXP)
