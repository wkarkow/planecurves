Quadratrix of Abdank-Abakanowicz

A transcendental curve, also called the circular integral, traced by Abdank-Abakanowicz’s integraph when the input is a circle; the output ordinate is the running integral of the circle’s vertical component.

Quadratrix of Abdank-Abakanowicz parametric equations:
\[ x = R\sin(t),\quad y = \dfrac{R^{2}}{2}\bigl(t + \sin(t)\cos(t)\bigr) \]
Equivalent Cartesian form:
\[ y = \dfrac{R^{2}}{2}\arcsin\dfrac{x}{R} + \dfrac{x}{2}\sqrt{R^{2}-x^{2}} \]

History and Applications

The Quadratrix of Abdank-Abakanowicz1, also known as the circular integral, is a transcendental curve generated by the integraph invented by Bruno Abdank-Abakanowicz (1852–1900). A Polish mathematician, inventor, and electrical engineer, he patented the integraph in 1880 as a mechanical device that traces the running integral of a given input curve; commercial versions were later produced by the Coradi firm in Zurich. He detailed the instrument, the integral curve concept, and practical uses in his 1886 book Les intégraphes, la courbe intégrale et ses applications.2

The quadratrix specifically arises when the integraph processes the parametric equations of a circle. With the basic calculus concept of integration as accumulated area, the device takes the circle’s vertical component and produces the output curve whose y-coordinate at each parameter value \(t\) equals the definite integral of that component.

The integraph found real-life use in engineering and the natural sciences for graphical computation of integrals before electronic computers existed. Engineers employed it to determine areas under irregular curves, moments of inertia, centers of gravity, and approximate solutions to certain differential equations in fields such as naval architecture (ship stability), structural mechanics, and physics.3

AutoLISP Code

(defun C:ABDANK (/ basept xaxisangle cd sa sb sm om os divcount cosangle sinangle x0 y0 plineents segcount baseinfo paramstep
                   divpt xspan restartflag id iq dcl opt kw mode dpr dct dpt span eq_display xc yc skm a1 a2 x1 y1 x2 y2 fa)
  (setq id "Quadratrix of Abdank-Abakanowicz"
        iq "x=R·sin(t), y=(R²/2)·(t+sin(t)·cos(t))"
        dcl nil
        opt nil
        kw nil
        mode 'polar
        dpr "# of divisions around circle"
        dct 360
        dpt nil
        span 360
        kw (ca id iq dcl opt kw mode dpr dct dpt span)
        fa (get_real 2 "R (scale)" 2.0)
        eq_display (strcat "\nR=" (rtos fa 2 4))
        xc '(+ (* 0.5 fa fa (+ (eval a2) (* (sin (eval a2)) (cos (eval a2))))) (* 0.25 pi fa fa))
        yc '(* fa (sin (eval a2)))
        skm '(T T T)
        a1 (/ pi -2.0)
        a2 a1
        x1 (eval xc)
        y1 (eval yc)
        a2 '(+ a1 paramstep)
        x2 '(eval xc)
        y2 '(eval yc))
  (cc))
(C:ABDANK)
