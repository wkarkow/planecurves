Parabola Pedal Curve

The locus of the feet of the perpendiculars drawn from a fixed pedal point \((x_{0},y_{0})\) to the tangents of a parabola.

Parabola Pedal Curve parametric equations:
\[ x = \dfrac{(x_{0}-a)\,t^{2}+y_{0}\,t}{t^{2}+1},\quad y = \dfrac{a\,t^{3}+x_{0}\,t+y_{0}}{t^{2}+1} \]

History and Applications

The Parabola Pedal Curve1,2,3 is the locus of the feet of the perpendiculars drawn from a fixed point (the pedal point) to the tangents of a parabola. The broader idea of pedal curves was investigated by Gilles Personne de Roberval in 1693 and Colin Maclaurin in 1718; Maclaurin’s 1718 work included explicit study of pedal curves of conics. The term “podaire” (from Greek for “foot”) was introduced by Olry Terquem in 1847. Detailed analysis of pedal curves of conic sections, including the parabola, appears in the two 1879 papers of A. Ameseder published in Archiv für Mathematik und Physik. Depending on the location of the pedal point relative to the parabola, the resulting curve takes familiar named forms: the cissoid of Diocles (pedal point at the vertex), a strophoid (pedal point at the foot of the perpendicular from focus to directrix), the Maclaurin trisectrix (pedal point on the directrix), or simply a straight line (pedal point at the focus). These reductions are properties of the algebraic geometry of the curve and are useful for recognizing limiting cases when the pedal point coincides with standard parabola features.

No real-life applications in engineering or the natural sciences appear in the standard references for this specific curve. It remains a classical object of study in differential geometry and algebraic curve theory.

(defun C:PARAPEDAL (/ basept xaxisangle cd sa sb sm om os divcount cosangle sinangle x0 y0 plineents segcount baseinfo divpt xstep paramstep
                      restartflag id iq dcl opt kw mode dpr dct dpt span eq_display xc yc skm a1 a2 x1 y1 x2 y2 fa fb fc )
  (setq id "Parabola Pedal Curve"
        iq "x=((x₀-a)·t²+y₀·t)/(t²+1), y=(a·t³+x₀·t+y₀)/(t²+1)"
        dcl nil
        opt nil
        kw nil
        mode 'polar
        dpr "# of divisions around circle"
        dct 360
        dpt nil
        span 360
        kw (ca id iq dcl opt kw mode dpr dct dpt span)
        fa (get_real 2 "a (parabola scale)" 1.0)
        fb (get_real 0 "x₀ (pedal point x-coordinate)" 1.0)
        fc (get_real 0 "y₀ (pedal point y-coordinate)" -1.0)
        paramstep (if (= (get_keyword 0 "Plus Minus" "\nSweep in Plus or Minus direction?" "Plus") "Minus") (- paramstep) paramstep)
        eq_display (strcat "\na = " (rtos fa 2 4) ", x₀ = " (rtos fb 2 4) ", y₀ = " (rtos fc 2 4))
        xc '(/ (+ (* (- fb fa) (* (eval a2) (eval a2))) (* fc (eval a2))) (+ (* (eval a2) (eval a2)) 1.0))
        yc '(/ (+ (* fa (* (eval a2) (eval a2)) (eval a2)) (* fb (eval a2)) fc) (+ (* (eval a2) (eval a2)) 1.0))
        skm '(T T T)
        a1 0
        a2 a1
        x1 (eval xc)
        y1 (eval yc)
        a2 '(+ a1 paramstep)
        x2 '(eval xc)
        y2 '(eval yc))
  (cc))
